## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配

1. `shaped like` 形状像……
2. `full of` 装满了……
3. `look at` 看……
4. `a room full of balls` 一个装满球的房间

### 二、语法知识点

1. 重复句型 — `Our house has a ____!`（全书核心句式，练习名词搭配）
2. 颜色形容词 — red, green, purple, colorful（修饰名词前置）
3. 数量表达 — `seven cats`（基数词 + 名词复数）
4. 感官动词 + 形容词 — `look scared` / `look surprised` / `look happy`

### 三、故事内容提问

1. What is the house shaped like?  **A:** A shoe.
2. What things does the house have inside?  **A:** Red floor, green walls, a room full of balls, seven cats, colorful hats, a slide, a train, and a purple pool.
3. How do the mice feel about their house?  **A:** They think it is so cool.
4. What is the story trying to tell us?  **A:** A house can be special and full of surprises even if it looks unusual — being different is cool.
