## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配

1. `be shaped like` 形状像……
2. `a round door` 一扇圆门
3. `a room full of balls` 装满球的房间
4. `a train you can ride` 可以乘坐的火车
5. `a purple pool` 紫色的泳池
6. `so cool` 太酷了

### 二、语法知识点

1. 核心句型 `Our house has + 名词`（第三人称单数 has）— `Our house has a round door!` / `Our house has seven cats!`
2. `be + 形容词` 表状态 — `The floor is red.` / `The walls are green.` / `Our house is so cool!`
3. 数量词 + 可数名词复数 — `seven cats` / `two mice`（注意 mouse → mice）
4. 形容词修饰名词 — `a round door` / `colorful hats` / `a purple pool`
5. `look + 形容词` 表看起来…… — `They look scared.` / `He looks surprised.`

### 三、故事内容提问

1. What is the house shaped like?  **A:** A shoe.
2. What does the house have inside and outside?  **A:** A round door, a red floor, green walls, a room full of balls, seven cats, colorful hats, a new slide, a train, and a purple pool.
3. Which part of the house would the mice like best?  **A:** Maybe the slide, the train, or the pool — they all look fun.
4. How do the mice feel about their house at the end?  **A:** They think it is so cool and they are proud of it.
5. What is the story trying to tell us?  **A:** A house can be special and full of surprises even if it looks unusual — being different is cool.
