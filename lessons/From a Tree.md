# 《From a Tree》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. What do you think this book is about?"
**A:** "A girl holding an apple. It's about a tree."

---

### (2/6) `came from a tree`

#### 💬
1. "What is the same about all these things?"  **A:** "They all came from a tree."
2. "Can you name something else that comes from a tree?"  **A:** "Paper / fruit / furniture / a house."
3. "Why do you think the book starts with this question?"  **A:** "To make us curious / to show us trees are important."

---

### (3/6) `timber` `build and make`

#### 💬
1. "What is timber?"  **A:** "Timber is wood."
2. "What is timber used for?"  **A:** "To build and make things."
3. "Look at the house frame. What is it made of?"  **A:** "Wood / timber. Do you think your house has wood inside?"

---

### (4/6) `forest` `logs`

#### 💬
1. "Where are the trees cut down?"  **A:** "In a forest."
2. "What are the trees cut into?"  **A:** "Logs."
3. "How do you feel about cutting down trees? Why?"  **A:** "Sad, because animals live there / But we need wood for houses."

---

### (5/6) `sawmill` `planks` `dried`

#### 💬
1. "Where do the logs go?"  **A:** "To a sawmill."
2. "What are most logs cut into?"  **A:** "Long planks."
3. "Why are the planks dried?"  **A:** "To make them strong / to remove water. What happens if wood is wet?"

---

### (6/6) `woodchips` `paper`

#### 💬
1. "What is paper made from?"  **A:** "Woodchips."
2. "What are woodchips?"  **A:** "Small bits of logs and timber scraps."
3. "Can you think of other things made from trees?"  **A:** "Pencils / furniture / chopsticks / doors. Try to save paper — it comes from trees!"

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `came from a tree` 来自一棵树
2. `Timber is used to build and make things` 木材被用来建造和制作东西
3. `cut down` 砍伐
4. `cut into logs` 切成原木
5. `be dried` 被晾干
6. `made from woodchips` 由木屑制成

### 二、语法知识点
1. 被动语态：`Timber is used to...` / `Trees are cut down` / `The planks are dried` / `Paper is made from...`
2. 一般现在时描述事实：`Paper is made from woodchips.`
3. 疑问词 + 不定式：`What is timber used for?`

### 三、故事内容提问
1. What do guitar, pencils, and timber have in common?  **A:** They all came from a tree.
2. What happens to trees in a forest?  **A:** They are cut down and cut into logs.
3. Where do logs go next?  **A:** To a sawmill.
4. How is paper made?  **A:** Logs and timber scraps are cut into small bits to make woodchips, then paper is made from woodchips.
