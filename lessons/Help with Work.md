## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配

1. `a lot of work` 许多工作
2. `work hard` 努力工作
3. `do heavy farm work` 做繁重的农活
4. `pull the plow through the soil` 拉犁翻土
5. `a good helper` 一个好帮手
6. `stay with` 陪伴……

### 二、语法知识点

1. `too` 表"也" — `Quin is a farmer, too.`（句末的 too 表示"也是"）
2. `help sb (to) do sth` — `The yak helps him do heavy farm work.`（help + 宾语 + 动词原形）
3. `what kind of...` 提问种类 — `What kind of work can a puppy do?`
4. `make sb + 形容词` — `It makes him very happy.`（使某人怎么样）

### 三、故事内容提问

1. What is a lot of work?  **A:** Growing plants.
2. How does the first farmer work in the field?  **A:** With his yak.
3. What does the yak help the farmer do?  **A:** Do heavy farm work.
4. What does Quin's ox pull through the soil?  **A:** The plow.
5. What can a puppy do?  **A:** It can be a good helper and a friend.
6. (推断性) How do the animals help the people in this book?  **A:** They do the heavy work and keep people company.
