### (2/5) `den`

> 左页封面（作者 Sui Jones），右页：Five bears are all in a den – Mom, Dad, and three cubs.

#### 💬
1. "Where is the bear family at the beginning of the story? What is a den like?"  **A:** "They are in a den — a warm, safe home in a tree or a hill."
2. "Who makes up this family?"  **A:** "Mom, Dad, and three cubs — five bears in all."
3. "(推断性) Why do you think bears sleep in a den instead of out in the open?"  **A:** "Because a den keeps them warm and dry, and other animals cannot easily get in."
4. "(推断性) The story starts and ends with the same sentence. Why do you think the writer does that?"  **A:** "To show that the day is over and the family is home safe again."
5. "Do you have a place that feels as warm and safe as a den? What is it like?"  **A:** "My bed with my blanket / my room, because it is quiet and mine."

### (3/5) `paw`

> 左页：Mom lifts one paw. Dad lifts two paws. All three cubs lift their paws.
> 右页：Mom walks to the river. Dad walks to the river. Three cubs run to the river.

#### 💬
1. "What does each bear do with its paws?"  **A:** "Mom lifts one paw, Dad lifts two, and all three cubs lift their paws too."
2. "(推断性) Nobody says a word on this page. Why do you think they lift their paws together?"  **A:** "Maybe it is a morning stretch / a way of saying good morning to each other."
3. "Where do they go next, and how does each bear get there?"  **A:** "They go to the river. Mom and Dad walk, and the three cubs run."
4. "(推断性) The parents walk but the cubs run. What does that tell you about the cubs?"  **A:** "They are full of energy and excited to get there / they cannot wait."
5. "When you go somewhere with your family, do you run ahead or walk with them? Why?"  **A:** "I run ahead because I'm excited / I walk with my mom so I don't get lost."

### (4/5) `trap` `climb`

> 左页：Mom traps one fish. Dad traps two fish. Three cubs trap three, four, five fish.
> 右页：Mom naps. Dad naps. Three cubs climb the tree.

#### 💬
1. "How many fish does each bear trap? Who traps the most?"  **A:** "Mom traps one and Dad traps two. The cubs trap three, four and five — three fish, so they trap the most."
2. "(推断性) Why does the book count the fish one, two, three, four, five instead of just saying 'five fish'?"  **A:** "To show each bear catching its own fish / to count together as a family."
3. "What do Mom and Dad do after fishing, and why?"  **A:** "They take a nap, because they are tired and full after eating."
4. "(推断性) Why do you think the cubs climb the tree instead of napping like their parents?"  **A:** "They still have lots of energy and want to play / climbing is fun for them."
5. "After a busy day, are you more like Mom and Dad or more like the cubs? Why?"  **A:** "Like the cubs — I still want to play / Like my parents — I'm tired and want to rest."

---

### (5/5) `call`

> 左页：Mom calls. Dad calls. Three cubs run, run, run!
> 右页：Five bears are all in a den – Mom, Dad, and three cubs.

#### 💬
1. "What happens at the end of the story?"  **A:** "Mom and Dad call, and the three cubs run, run, run back to them."
2. "(推断性) Why do you think the parents call the cubs at that moment?"  **A:** "Because it is time to go home / it is getting dark / dinner is ready."
3. "The book says 'run, run, run' three times. Why not just 'run'?"  **A:** "To show they run fast and hurry home / they are far away and run a long way."
4. "(推断性) The last page repeats the first page. What does that tell you about the bears' day?"  **A:** "The day went full circle — they went out together and came home together, safe."
5. "If you were one of the cubs, which part of the day would be your favorite? Why?"  **A:** "Trapping fish, because it is exciting / Climbing the tree, because it is fun and high."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配

1. `in a den` 在洞穴里
2. `lift a paw` 抬起一只爪子
3. `walk to the river` 走到河边
4. `trap fish` 抓鱼
5. `take a nap` 小睡一会儿
6. `climb the tree` 爬树
7. `run, run, run` 跑呀跑（重复表示一直跑、跑得急）
8. `time to go home` 该回家了
9. 动物幼崽：`bear-cub` 熊崽，`cat-kitten` 小猫，`dog-puppy` 小狗，`pig-piglet` 小猪，`duck-duckling` 小鸭，`kangaroo-joey` 小袋鼠

### 二、语法知识点

1. **数字 one–five** — one paw / two paws / three, four, five fish；fish 单复数同形。
2. **一般现在时，第三人称单数** — Mom **lifts** one paw. Dad **walks** to the river. Mom **naps**. Mom **calls**. 主语是一个人时动词加 -s。
3. **复数主语用动词原形** — Three cubs **run** to the river. They **climb** the tree. All three cubs **lift** their paws.
4. **All + 数量 + 名词** — **All three** cubs lift their paws. 表示"三个全都"。
5. **more / fewer 比较** — The cubs trap **more** fish than Mom. Mom traps **fewer** fish than the cubs.
6. **动词重复表强调** — They run, run, run! 重复让画面更有动感，读出时一次比一次快。
7. **介词搭配** — **in** a den（里面），**to** the river（去河边），**up** the tree（爬上树）。

### 三、故事内容提问

1. Who are the five bears?  **A:** Mom, Dad, and three cubs.
2. What does the family do first, and what do they do next?  **A:** First they lift their paws, then they walk or run to the river.
3. How many fish does the family trap all together?  **A:** Five — Mom's one fish, Dad's two, and the cubs' three, four and five.
4. What do Mom and Dad do while the cubs climb the tree?  **A:** They take a nap.
5. Why do the cubs run home at the end?  **A:** Because Mom and Dad call them — it is time to go back to the den.
6. What is the story mainly about?  **A:** A bear family spending a day together — working, resting, and coming home safe.
7. Can you retell the whole day in three sentences?  **A:** "Five bears wake up in their den. They go to the river and trap fish, then Mom and Dad nap while the cubs climb. When the parents call, the cubs run home."
