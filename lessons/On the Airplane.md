# 《On the Airplane》

> 级别：C ｜ 时长：15 分钟 ｜ 平台共 5 页，截到 5 张，无缺页。C 级、15 分钟短课：问题短、答案给完整句型支架，多用指认+跟读，推断题只留 1–2 个简单的。

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. Where is the girl?"
**A:** "She is on an airplane. She is sitting by the window."
#### 💬 "What can she see from the window?"
**A:** "She can see small houses and green fields."
#### 💬 "Have you been on an airplane? Where did you go?"
**A:** "Yes, I have. I went to Beijing. / No, I haven't. I want to go to Shanghai."

### (2/5) `big seat` `go up`

#### 💬
1. "The girl sits in a `big seat`. Is her seat small or big?"  **A:** "Her seat is big."
2. "She says, 'Go up! Go up!' What goes up?"  **A:** "The airplane goes up."
3. "What can you see at the airport?"  **A:** "I can see many airplanes."
4. "(推断性) How does the girl feel? Happy or scared?"  **A:** "She is happy. She is smiling."

### (3/5) `small houses`

#### 💬
1. "`Look at the small houses.` Are the houses really small?"  **A:** "No. They look small because the airplane is high."
2. "What color are the houses?"  **A:** "They are red, white, and brown."
3. "What else can you see in the sky?"  **A:** "I can see white clouds."
4. "What looks small from an airplane?"  **A:** "Cars and people look small, too."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `on the airplane` 在飞机上
2. `sit in a seat` 坐在座位上
3. `go up` 上升 / 起飞
4. `go down` 下降 / 降落
5. `look at` 看……
6. `eat and sleep` 吃饭和睡觉
7. `come and hug me` 过来抱抱我

### 二、语法知识点
1. **一般现在时第一人称** — **I sit** in a big seat. / **I eat and sleep** on the airplane. 主语是 I，动词用原形。
2. **祈使句** — **Look at** the small houses. / **Go up!** / **Come and hug** me. 祈使句用动词原形开头，省略主语 you。
3. **big / small 反义词** — a **big** seat ↔ **small** houses；注意：房子看起来小是因为飞机飞得高，不是因为房子真的变小了。
4. **and 连接并列动词** — I **eat and sleep**…；两个动作共用一个主语。
5. **Here's…** — **Here's Grandma!** = Here is Grandma，表示“奶奶在这儿”。

### 三、故事内容提问
1. Where does the girl sit?  **A:** "In a big seat on the airplane."
2. What does she say when the airplane goes up?  **A:** "Go up! Go up!"
3. What looks small from the airplane?  **A:** "The houses look small."
4. What two things does she do on the airplane?  **A:** "She eats and sleeps."
5. Who does she see at the end?  **A:** "She sees Grandma."
6. What is the book mainly about?  **A:** "A girl's trip on an airplane to see her grandma."
