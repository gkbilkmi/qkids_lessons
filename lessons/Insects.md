## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配

1. `all over the world` 全世界
2. `hot / cold places` 热的地方 / 冷的地方
3. `on land` 在陆地上
4. `body parts` 身体部位
5. `work together as a team` 像团队一样一起工作
6. `lay eggs` 产卵

### 二、语法知识点

1. 一般现在时复数主语 — `Insects live all over the world.`（复数主语动词用原形）
2. `some / most / many` 作限定词 — `Some insects are big.` / `Most insects lay eggs.` / `Many insects have wings.`（表达"部分/大多数/许多"）
3. `so... (that)` 结构（书中简化版）— `Some insects are so small, you need a microscope to see them.`（如此之小）
4. 一般将来时 — `This egg will hatch.` / `One day the caterpillar will be a moth!`（will + 动词原形）

### 三、故事内容提问

1. Where do insects live?  **A:** All over the world — hot and cold places, land and water.
2. How many body parts and legs do insects have?  **A:** Three body parts and six legs.
3. What do many insects have on their backs?  **A:** Wings.
4. How do bees live and work?  **A:** In a hive, working together as a team.
5. What comes out when an insect egg hatches?  **A:** A caterpillar.
6. What will the caterpillar become one day?  **A:** A moth.
