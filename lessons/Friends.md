## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `make friends with` 和……交朋友
2. `hold hands` 手拉手
3. `play baseball` 打棒球
4. `under a tree` 在树下
5. `at the beach` 在海滩上
6. `cheer with pom-poms` 拿着啦啦球加油
7. `four eyes` 四只眼睛（也常用来开玩笑说戴眼镜的人）

### 二、语法知识点
1. **一般现在时 be 动词** — Some friends **are** big / small / tall / brown / black. 主语是复数，用 are。
2. **have 表示"有"** — Some friends **have** black eyes / blue eyes / four eyes. 主语是复数，用 have。
3. **are vs. have** — `are` 后面接形容词（are big），`have` 后面接名词（have black eyes）。
4. **形容词对比** — big ↔ small，tall ↔ short；C 级可带出比较级：taller / smaller。
5. **everyone** 作主语表示"每个人"，后面跟单数动词：Everyone **is** different.

### 三、故事内容提问
1. What does the book say about big and small friends?  **A:** "Some friends are big, and some friends are small."
2. What color eyes do some friends have?  **A:** "Some have black eyes, some have blue eyes, and one has four eyes."
3. Who is the last friend on page 10?  **A:** "A green alien with four eyes."
4. What does the boy say at the end?  **A:** "I make friends with everyone."
5. What is the big idea of this book?  **A:** "Friends can look different, but everyone can be a friend."
