## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `wave to a friend` 向朋友挥手
2. `on my walk` 在我散步的时候
3. `in my tree` 在我的树上（常去、像自己专属的地方）
4. `at the store` 在商店
5. `in the mirror` 在镜子里
6. `that's me` 那是我

### 二、核心句型
1. `I wave to a friend + 地点` 我在……向一个朋友挥手
2. `Who is...?` 谁在……？

### 三、语法知识点
1. 一般现在时（表示习惯性、反复发生的动作）
   - 例词：wave, walk, play
   - 例句：I wave to a friend on my walk.
2. 方位介词 in / on / at
   - 例词：in my tree, on my walk, at the store, in the mirror
   - 例句：I wave to a friend in my tree. / I wave to a friend at the store.
3. 指示代词 that
   - 例句：That's me!（那是我！）

### 四、故事内容提问
1. Who is the story about?（故事讲的是谁？）
   **A:** A baby who waves to friends everywhere.
2. Where does the baby wave to a friend on his walk?（散步时宝宝在哪里向朋友挥手？）
   **A:** On the street, next to a park with a fence.
3. Who is in the baby's tree?（"宝宝的树"上有谁？）
   **A:** His dad, a bird, and a dog.
4. How do the two babies say hello at the store?（两个宝宝在商店里怎么打招呼？）
   **A:** They wave to each other from their strollers.
5. Who is the last friend the baby waves to?（宝宝最后一个"朋友"是谁？）
   **A:** Himself, in the mirror.
