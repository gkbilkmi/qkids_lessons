# 《Feeding Time》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. We see three sheep eating grass. What do you think this book is about?"
**A:** "It's about animals eating food. / It's about feeding time on the farm."

### (2/5) `chickens`
#### 💬
1. "What animals are on this page? What are they eating?"
**A:** "They are chickens. They eat grain."
2. (推断性) "Why do chickens like to eat grain?"
**A:** "Because grain gives them energy. / Because grain is yummy for chickens."
3. "Do you like chickens? Have you ever fed chickens?"
**A:** "Yes, I like chickens. / Yes, I fed chickens at the farm."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `chickens`
2. `sheep` 绵羊
3. `horses` 马（复数）
4. `cows` 奶牛（复数）

### 二、核心句型
1. `Do you like + 名词?` 你喜欢……吗？
2. `What do you + 动词?` 你……什么？
3. `Can you + 动词原形?` 你能……吗？
4. `I can + 动词原形` 我能……
5. `What are + 名词?` ……是什么？

### 三、语法知识点
1. 现在进行时
   - 例词：eating, sleeping
   - 例句：They are eating grass.
   - 例句：They are sleeping. It is night.
2. 一般现在时（三单）
   - 例词：is, gives
   - 例句：Because grain gives them energy. / Because grain is yummy for chickens.
   - 例句：They are sleeping. It is night.
3. 一般过去时
   - 例词：saw
   - 例句：Yes, I saw a horse at the zoo. / Yes, I like horses.
4. 情态动词 can / could（表能力）
   - 例词：can, could
   - 例句：Yes, I like sheep. I can see white sheep and one black sheep.

### 四、故事内容提问
1. Why do chickens like to eat grain?
2. Why are carrots good for horses?
3. Why are the cows still asleep while the other animals are eating?
