## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配

1. `tap at the door` 敲门（轻敲）
2. `pat at snowballs` 拍雪球
3. `have my cap` 拿走我的帽子（给人东西时的口语：Tam, have my cap.）
4. `make a snowman` 堆雪人

### 二、语法知识点

1. 一般现在时第三人称单数 — `Tam taps at my door.`（主语三单，动词加 -s）
2. `tap` 与 `pat` 的词族与拟声重复 — `Tap, tap, tap.` / `pat, pat, pat`（重复表节奏和动作持续）
3. 形容词用法 — `Tam is cold.`（be + 形容词表状态）

### 三、故事内容提问

1. Who knocks at the door at the start?  **A:** Tam.
2. How does Tam feel when she comes in?  **A:** She is cold.
3. What does the boy give Tam?  **A:** His cap.
4. What do the kids do outside together?  **A:** They pat snowballs and tap at a snowman.
5. How do the kids feel at the end of the story?  **A:** Happy — they like the snow.
6. (推断性) Why is the story called "Winter Fun"?  **A:** Because playing in the snow in winter is fun.
