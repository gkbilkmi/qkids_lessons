**A:** "She wants to make some bread." / "She wants to find friends to help her."

#### 💬

### (3/4) `bread`
#### 💬
1. "What does the hen do when nobody helps?"
**A:** "She makes the bread all by herself."
2. "How does the bread look and smell?"
**A:** "It looks yummy and smells good. She says 'Mmm! Yum bread!'"
3. "(推断性) How do you think the hen feels making the bread alone?"
**A:** "She feels a little tired, but also happy because the bread is yummy."

### (4/4) `share`
#### 💬
1. "When the bread is ready, who wants to eat it?"
**A:** "The cat and the dog want to eat it. They say 'I can! I can!'"
2. "What does the hen say to them?"
**A:** "She says 'Sorry. No help, no bread!'"
3. "(推断性) What does this story teach us?"
**A:** "If you don't help, you don't get to share." / "We should help our friends."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `help me` 帮我
2. `I am busy` 我很忙
3. `make bread` 做面包
4. `Yum bread` 好香的面包
5. `no help, no bread` 不帮忙，就没面包吃

### 二、核心句型
1. `Can you help me?` 你能帮我吗？
2. `I can do it!` 我能自己做！
3. `Sorry. No help, no bread!` 抱歉，没帮忙就别想吃面包！

### 三、语法知识点
1. 一般现在时（描述故事里的动作）
   - 例词：help, ask, say, make, want, eat
   - 例句：The little red hen asks, "Can you help me?"
2. 情态动词 can / can't
   - 例词：can, can't (no)
   - 例句：I can do it! / No help, no bread.

4. How does the bread smell?
