# 《A Yam in a Cup》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. What do you think this story is about?"
**A:** "I think it's about growing a plant in a cup. / A girl puts a yam in water."

---

### (2/5) `in a cup or a mug`

#### 💬
1. "Where does the girl put the yam?"
   **A:** "In a cup or a mug."
2. "What is in the cup?"
   **A:** "Water."
3. "Do you have a cup or a mug at home? What do you drink in it?"
   **A:** "Yes, I do. / I drink milk (water, juice) in it."

---

### (3/5) *keep + sth + adj*

#### 💬
1. "How does the girl look after the yam?"
   **A:** "She keeps the yam very wet in the cup and keeps it in the sun."
2. "Why does the yam need water and sun? (推断性)"
   **A:** "Because plants need water and sun to grow."
3. "What do you water at home — flowers, a tree, or grass?"
   **A:** "I water the flowers. / I don't water anything at home."

---

### (4/5) `bud`

#### 💬
1. "What does the yam have on a bud?"
   **A:** "A bug."
2. "What does the girl say to the boy with the scissors?"
   **A:** "Do not cut that bud!"
3. "Is there a 'do not...' rule at your home? What is it?"
   **A:** "Yes — do not touch the stove. / Do not run in the room."

---

### (5/5) *What can you grow in a cup?*

#### 💬
1. "The girl grows yams in cups. What can you grow in a cup?"
   **A:** "I can grow beans / peas / a small flower in a cup."
2. "Why does the girl grow yams — to eat them or for fun? (推断性)"
   **A:** "For fun. The book says 'it is for the fun of it!'"
3. "Would you like to try this experiment? What would you need?"
   **A:** "Yes, I would. / I need a cup, water, and a seed."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `put the yam in a cup or a mug`：把山药放进杯子或马克杯里
2. `keep the yam very wet`：让山药保持湿润
3. `in the sun`：在阳光下
4. `on a bud`：在芽上
5. `do not cut`：不要剪
6. `for the fun of it`：为了好玩

### 二、语法知识点
1. "Put the yam in a cup." / "Keep the yam very wet." 祈使句省略主语 you，表示指令；`put / keep + 宾语 + 地点/状态`。
2. "Keep the yam very wet." `keep + 宾语 + 形容词` 表示"让……保持某种状态"，wet 是形容词"湿的"。
3. "Do not cut that bud!" `do not + 动词原形`构成否定祈使句，表示禁止："不要做……"。
4. "When I grow yams in cups..." `when` 引导时间状语从句，表示"当……的时候"。

### 三、故事内容提问
1. What does the girl put in the cup?
   **A:** "A yam."
2. How does she look after the yam?
   **A:** "She keeps it very wet and keeps it in the sun."
3. What is on the bud?
   **A:** "A bug."
4. Why does the girl say "Do not cut that bud"?
   **A:** "Because the bud is growing and she wants it to live."
5. Why does the girl grow yams in cups?
   **A:** "For the fun of it."
6. What does she ask the reader at the end?
   **A:** "What can you grow in a cup?"
