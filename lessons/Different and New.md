# 《Different and New》

**A:** "I think it's about two kids, Tex and Indi, and maybe a new place like a camp or school." (任何合理预测都可以)

### (2/5) `on their way to`

#### 💬
1. "Where are Tex and Indi going? Who is with them?"
   **A:** "They are on their way to day camp. Dad is with them (driving)."
2. "What did they think about camp last summer? What does Indi hope?"
   **A:** "They loved camp last summer. Indi hopes everything is exactly the same."
3. "What does Dad say about different and new?"
   **A:** "'Some things will be the same and some things will be different — just remember, different and new can be fun, too.'"
4. "(推断性) Indi hopes everything will be exactly the same. What does that tell us about Indi?"
   **A:** "She likes familiar things / she is a little nervous about new things." (合理即可)
5. "Do you like trying new things? What new thing would you like to try?"
   **A:** "Yes/No. I would like to try ...." (自由回答)

### (3/5) `counselor`

#### 💬

   **A:** "The counselors were all new, and so was the rec center."

   **A:** "She feels nervous and says, 'Yikes, everything looks different. My stomach hurts. I think I need to go home.'"

   **A:** "A friendly teenager wearing a silly hat — their camp counselor, Jumping Jack."
4. "(推断性) Does Indi's stomach really hurt? Why does she feel that way?"
   **A:** "Not really — she is nervous and scared of all the new things." (合理即可)
5. "Have you ever felt nervous on a first day — like the first day of a new class? How did you feel in your body?"
   **A:** "Yes/No. My tummy felt funny / my heart beat fast." (自由回答)

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `on their way to`：在去……的路上
2. `day camp`：日间夏令营
3. `exactly the same`：一模一样
4. `look around`：环顾四周
5. `rec center`：(recreation center) 活动中心
6. `just in time for`：正好赶上（You're just in time for the name game!）
7. `form a circle`：围成一个圈
8. `back and forth`：来回地（toss a ball back and forth 把球抛来抛去）
9. `before long`：不久之后
10. `pick up`：（开车）接（某人）
11. `have a stomachache`：肚子疼/胃疼

### 二、语法知识点
1. `Tex and Indi were on their way to day camp.` — 过去式 were，on one's way to + 地点。
2. `I hope everything is exactly the same.` — hope + (that) 从句，从句用一般现在时。
3. `The counselors were all new, and so was the rec center.` — "so + be 动词 + 主语"表示"(另一个人/物)也是"。
4. `Before long, Indi forgot (that) she ever had a stomachache.` — before long 时间状语"不久"，宾语从句省略 that。
5. `Different and new can be fun, too.` — 情态动词 can + be + 形容词，"也可能是有趣的"；两个形容词用 and 连用作主语。

### 三、故事内容提问
1. Where were Tex and Indi going at the start of the story?
   **A:** "To day camp."
2. Why did Indi want to go home?
   **A:** "Because everything looked different, she didn't know anyone, and her stomach hurt — she was nervous."
3. (推断性) Who was Jumping Jack?
   **A:** "Their friendly camp counselor, a teenager in a silly hat."
4. What did the campers do in the name game?
   **A:** "They formed a circle, called out their names, and tossed a big beach ball back and forth."
5. When did Indi forget her stomachache?
   **A:** "Before long, while she was laughing and playing."
6. What did Tex and Indi tell Mom at the end?
   **A:** "'Fantastic!' said Tex. 'Super fantastic — Dad was right, different and new can be fun!'"
