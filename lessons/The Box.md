# 《The Box》


#### 💬 "Look at the cover. A girl is standing next to a big box. What do you think she will do with it?"
**A:** "Maybe she will make something with it / play in it."

---

### (2/5) `gave` `a big box`

#### 💬
1. "Who gave Kim the box, and how big is it?"  **A:** "Mom gave it to her. It is a big box."
2. "(推断性) Why do you think Mom gave Kim an empty box instead of a toy?"  **A:** "Because an empty box is more fun — you can make anything with it."
3. "What is the biggest box you have ever played with?"  **A:** "A box from the ___ / I played with a big box at home."

---

### (3/5) `make a home` `rugs`

#### 💬
1. "What does Kim say she can do with the box?"  **A:** "She says, 'I can make a home!'"
2. "Meg brings something for the home. What is it, and where does it go?"  **A:** "She brings rugs. The rugs can go in here."
3. "How is this box home different from your real home?"  **A:** "It is much smaller / It has no bed or kitchen, but it is fun."

---

### (4/5) `a big cat`

#### 💬
1. "What does Bud bring, and where does it go?"  **A:** "He brings a big cat. The big cat can go in here."
2. "(推断性) Do you think a real cat would like to stay in this box home? Tell me why."  **A:** "No, a real cat likes to run around / Maybe yes, if there is a soft rug."
3. "Which is more fun to build a home in — a big box or a tent? Why?"  **A:** "A ___ is more fun, because ___."

---

### (5/5) `five red cups` `We had fun.`

#### 💬
1. "What does Nan bring into the box home, and how many are there?"  **A:** "She brings five red cups."
2. "(推断性) Kim made the home, but all her friends brought things too. Why do you think they did that?"  **A:** "Because they want to help her / They like making it together."
3. "The children all say, 'We had fun.' Is a game more fun alone or with friends? Why?"  **A:** "With friends, because we can share and laugh together."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配

1. `a big box` 一个大箱子
2. `make a home` 造一个家
3. `go in here` 放进来；进到这里
4. `five red cups` 五个红杯子
5. `have fun` 玩得开心

### 二、语法知识点

1. 一般过去时 give→gave / say→said / have→had — `Mom gave Kim a big box.` / `Meg said, "I have rugs."` / `We had fun.`（书中原句）
2. have 表示"有" — `I have rugs.` / `I have five red cups.`
3. can + 动词原形，表示"能、可以" — `I can make a home!` / `The rugs can go in here.`
4. 名词变复数 — rug→rugs / cup→cups

### 三、故事内容提问

1. What did Mom give Kim?  **A:** She gave Kim a big box.
2. What did Kim say she could make?  **A:** She said, "I can make a home!"
3. What did Meg bring for the home?  **A:** She brought rugs.
4. What did Bud have?  **A:** He had a big cat.
5. What did Nan bring, and how many?  **A:** She brought five red cups.
6. How did the children feel at the end?  **A:** They had fun.
