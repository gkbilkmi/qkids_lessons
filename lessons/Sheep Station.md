# 《Sheep Station》

> 级别：G ｜ 时长：15 分钟 ｜ 平台共 8 页，截到 8 张，无缺页。G 级非虚构科普：少问指认题，多问流程顺序、因果和比较；15 分钟课时紧，每页挑 3 题即可。

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. What is this place, and what is the man doing?"
**A:** "It's a sheep station — a very big sheep farm. The man is riding a quad bike and moving the sheep together, and a dog is helping him."
#### 💬 "What do you think a 'station' means here? Is it like a train station?"
**A:** "No. In Australia and New Zealand, a station is a huge farm for sheep or cows."
#### 💬 "Have you ever touched a sheep? How does its wool feel?"
**A:** "Yes, at a zoo. Its wool feels soft, thick, and warm."

### (2/8) `spring` `shear`

#### 💬
1. "It is `spring`. What job is it time to do on the station?"  **A:** "It is time to shear the sheep — to cut off their wool."
2. "Why do farmers shear sheep in spring, not in winter?"  **A:** "In winter the sheep need their wool to stay warm. In spring the weather gets warmer, so they don't need such a thick coat."
3. "(推断性) Look at the photo on the right. About how many sheep are there? Why are there so many?"  **A:** "There are hundreds. A station is very big, and wool from many sheep can be sold."

---

### (3/8) `hills` `look for`

#### 💬
1. "Where do the farmers ride to `look for` the sheep?"  **A:** "They ride up into the hills."
2. "Why is it sometimes hard to find them all?"  **A:** "The hills are big and the sheep spread out everywhere. Some may hide behind trees or hills."
3. "How do the farmers travel in the hills — find two ways in the photos."  **A:** "They ride horses, and they also ride quad bikes."
4. "(推断性) What could happen if a few sheep are left in the hills?"  **A:** "They won't be shorn, and they may get too hot in summer, or get lost."

---

### (4/8) `dogs` `get together`

#### 💬
1. "What work do the dogs do on the station?"  **A:** "They help the farmers get all the sheep together."
2. "How do the dogs move the sheep without hurting them?"  **A:** "They run around the flock and bark, so the sheep move the way the dogs want."
3. "(推断性) Why can't the farmers do this job alone?"  **A:** "There are too many sheep and the land is too big. The dogs are faster and can run all day."
4. "What other animals help people work?"  **A:** "Horses help farmers, and guide dogs help blind people."

---

### (5/8) `shearers` `shearing shed`

#### 💬
1. "Who are the `shearers`, and where do they work?"  **A:** "Shearers are the workers who cut the wool. They work inside the shearing shed."
2. "Describe how a shearer holds the sheep."  **A:** "He holds the sheep between his legs and cuts the wool off in one big piece."
3. "(推断性) Is shearing an easy job? What makes it hard?"  **A:** "No. Sheep are heavy and keep moving, and the shearer must be fast but careful not to cut the skin."

---

### (6/8) `cooler` `summer`

#### 💬
1. "After the shearing, where do the sheep go?"  **A:** "They go back up into the hills."
2. "Why will the sheep be `cooler` in summer now?"  **A:** "Their thick wool is gone, so the summer heat is easier for them."
3. "Compare the sheep before and after shearing. What changed?"  **A:** "Before, they were fat and fluffy with thick wool. After, they look thin and short-haired."
4. "What do you do to stay cool in summer?"  **A:** "I wear short sleeves, drink cold water, and stay in the shade."

---

### (7/8) `bales` `mill` `yarn`

#### 💬
1. "Where do the `bales of wool` go from the station?"  **A:** "They go to the mill — the wool factory."
2. "What three things do people do to the wool at the mill?"  **A:** "They clean it, dye it, and spin it into yarn."
3. "Put the steps in order: sheep in the hills → ? → ? → yarn."  **A:** "Farmers gather the sheep, shearers shear them, trucks take the bales to the mill, and workers clean, dye, and spin the wool."
4. "(推断性) Why is the wool dyed before it is made into clothes?"  **A:** "So people can have clothes in many colors, not only white and grey."

---

### (8/8) `made with wool`

#### 💬
1. "What four things in the picture are `made with wool`?"  **A:** "A rug, gloves, woollen socks, and a carpet."
2. "Which of these keeps you warm, and which is for the floor?"  **A:** "Gloves and socks keep me warm. The rug and carpet are for the floor."
3. "(推断性) Why is wool good for winter clothes?"  **A:** "Wool is thick and holds warm air, so it keeps the body warm."
4. "Do you have anything made of wool at home? What is it?"  **A:** "Yes, I have a wool sweater / scarf. It is very warm in winter."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `sheep station` （澳/新）大型牧羊场
2. `It is time to do sth.` 到做某事的时候了
3. `ride up into the hills` 骑行上山
4. `look for` 寻找
5. `get all the sheep together` 把羊聚到一起
6. `a lot of work to do` 有很多活要干
7. `bales of wool` 一捆捆羊毛
8. `spin it into yarn` 把它纺成毛线
9. `be made with` 由……制成（强调材料）

### 二、语法知识点
1. **一般现在时表事实** — The farmers **ride** up into the hills. / The dogs **help** the farmers. 科普文用一般现在时讲常态流程。
2. **主语 + be + 形容词比较级** — They will now be **cooler** when summer comes. cooler = cool 的比较级。
3. **并列动词列举** — People **clean** the wool, **dye** it and **spin** it into yarn. 三个动词并列，时态一致。
4. **too 的用法** — The dogs have a lot of work to do, **too**. too 放句末表示“也”。
5. **when 时间状语从句** — **When the shearing is over**, all the sheep go back. 主句将来/现在，从句用一般现在时表将来。
6. **sheep 单复数同形** — one sheep / all the sheep，sheep 的复数不加 s。

### 三、故事内容提问
1. In which season do farmers shear the sheep?  **A:** "In spring."
2. Who helps the farmers get the sheep together?  **A:** "The dogs."
3. Where do the shearers work?  **A:** "Inside the shearing shed."
4. Why are the sheep cooler after shearing?  **A:** "Because their thick wool is cut off before summer."
5. What happens to the wool at the mill?  **A:** "People clean it, dye it, and spin it into yarn."
6. What is the book mainly about?  **A:** "How wool gets from the sheep on a station to the things we use."
