# 《Dan and Sid》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. Two elephants are standing side by side. What do you think this story is about?"
**A:** "Two elephant friends and what they do together."

---

### (2/8) `I am ___`

#### 💬
1. "What does the elephant on the right say?"  **A:** "I am Dan."
2. "And what does the elephant on the left say?"  **A:** "I am Sid."
3. "Do you have a best friend? What is your friend's name?"  **A:** "Yes, my best friend is ___."

### (3/8) `Hello!`

#### 💬
1. "What do the two friends say to each other here?"  **A:** "Hello, Dan! / Hello, Sid!"
2. "How do they look when they say hello — happy or sad?"  **A:** "Happy / They smile and wave."
3. "When you meet your friend in the morning, what do you say?"  **A:** "I say hello / good morning!"

### (4/8) `sad` `mad`

#### 💬
1. "Look at these two. How does Sid feel, and how does Dan feel?"  **A:** "Sid is sad and Dan is mad."
2. "(推断性) Why do you think they feel that way?"  **A:** "Maybe they had a little fight / Maybe they didn't share."
3. "When do you feel sad or mad? What do you do then?"  **A:** "I feel sad when ___. I talk to my mom."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配

1. `I am ___` 我是……
2. `Hello!` 你好！
3. `have a plan` 有一个计划
4. `go out to play` 出去玩儿
5. `mix up` 弄混；搞乱

### 二、语法知识点

1. be 动词 am / is 与人称 — `I am Dan.` / `Sid is sad.` / `Dan is mad.`（书中原句）
2. 主 + be + 形容词 描述状态 — `Sid is sad.` / `Dan is red.` / `Sid is green.`
3. 一般现在时 描述状态与习惯 — `They have a plan.` / `They go out to play.`
4. Who 引导的特殊疑问句 — `Who mixed up Dan and Sid?`

### 三、故事内容提问

1. What are the two elephants' names?  **A:** Dan and Sid.
2. How do Sid and Dan feel at first?  **A:** Sid is sad and Dan is mad.
3. What is their plan?  **A:** They paint Sid green and Dan red.
4. What do they do one day?  **A:** They go out to play.
5. Who mixed up Dan and Sid?  **A:** The monkey.
6. Why do you think they paint themselves different colors?  **A:** So their friends can tell Dan and Sid apart.
