#### 💬 "Look at the cover. What do you think this story is about?"

### (2/9) `smooth` `rough`

#### 💬
1. "Look at the table. What things can you see?"
   **A:** "A pineapple, a balloon, a ball, an apple, a leaf, bark, a pebble and a rock."

   **A:** "The balloon is smooth."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `smooth` / `rough`：光滑的 / 粗糙的（一对反义词）
2. `touch and feel`：摸一摸、感受
3. `Which is smooth? Which is rough?`：哪个是光滑的？哪个是粗糙的？
4. `hold an egg`：拿着一个鸡蛋

### 二、语法知识点
1. `The apple is smooth.` — The + 名词 + is + 形容词，描述物品触感的句型；本书反复操练。
2. `a / an / the`：a pineapple, an apple（元音前用 an），特指时用 the。
3. 特殊疑问词 `Which`：Which is smooth? 用于在几个东西里做选择。

### 三、故事内容提问
1. What is smooth in the book?
   **A:** "The apple, the balloon, the leaf, the pebble (and the egg)."
2. What is rough in the book?
   **A:** "The pineapple, the ball, the bark, the rock."
3. (推断性) How do the children in the book know if something is smooth or rough?
   **A:** "They touch the things with their hands."
4. Which smooth thing and which rough thing go together on a page?
   **A:** "An apple and a pineapple / a balloon and a ball / a leaf and bark / a pebble and a rock."
5. Is the egg smooth or rough?
   **A:** "It's smooth."
6. What smooth or rough things can you find at home?
   **A:** "Smooth: table, glass, banana. Rough: towel, carpet, tree bark." (自由回答)
