# 《A House for Mouse》

#### 💬

### (3/7) `too-tall`

#### 💬
1. "What is too tall for Mouse? Who lives up in the tree?"
   **A:** "The house in the tree is too tall for Mouse. A bird lives in the tree."

2. "Why does Mouse jump down from the tree house? (推断性)"
   **A:** "Because the house is too tall and she can't reach or fit inside it, so she leaves."

3. "In your life, when is a house too tall for you? When you are small, what can you not reach?"
   **A:** "When I am small I cannot reach the kitchen cupboard. My dad's house is too tall for me."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `need a house`：需要一所房子
2. `a house for Mouse`：属于小老鼠的房子
3. `too + 形容词`（too small / too tall / too hot / too cold / too wet）：太……了
4. `just right`：刚刚好、正合适
5. `look at`：看
6. `home sweet home`：家是温馨的家（家就是最舒服的地方）

### 二、语法知识点
1. `need` 的用法：`Mouse needs a house.` 主语是第三人称单数 Mouse，所以 needs 要加 -s。
2. `too + 形容词` 表示"太……了"，否定意味：too small = 太小了（放不下）。本书每页一句 `This house is too + 形容词.`，是最核心的句型。
3. `for` 表所属：`This house is too cold for Mouse.` 意思是"这所房子对小老鼠来说太冷了"。
4. `is just right`：系动词 is + 副词短语，表示状态正好。
5. 感叹句表达：`Look at the cover.` 是祈使句，告诉孩子"看封面"。

### 三、故事内容提问
1. What does Mouse need?
   **A:** "She needs a house."

2. Why is the first house not good for Mouse?
   **A:** "Because it is too small."

3. What is too tall, too hot, too cold and too wet for Mouse?
   **A:** "The tree house is too tall, the house by the fire is too hot, the refrigerator is too cold, and the pond is too wet."

4. Who lives in the tree and in the pond?
   **A:** "A bird lives in the tree, and a frog lives in the pond."

5. What does Mouse's new house look like?
   **A:** "It has a red roof, a bed, a rug, a table and a 'HOME SWEET HOME' sign."

6. Why is the last house just right for Mouse?
   **A:** "Because it is not too small, too tall, too hot, too cold or too wet. It is just right."
