## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `See me hop/fly/jump...`：看我（做某事）～ 用于展示动作
2. `I can ..., too.`：我也会～
3. `jump and hop`：又跳又蹦

### 二、语法知识点
1. `can + 动词原形` 表能力：I can hop / I can swim。
2. `too` 副词，"也"，通常放在句尾：I can fly, too. I can swim, too.
3. `said the rabbit`：直接引语 + "said the + 角色" 倒装写法，读到 said 语气放松。

### 三、故事内容提问
1. How do the rabbit and the kangaroo move?
   **A:** "They hop."
2. Who says "See me fly"? 
   **A:** "The butterfly (says it)."
3. What does the frog say at the end?
   **A:** "I can swim, too. See!"
4. (推断性) Why do you think the frog says "See!" at the end of the story?
   **A:** "Because he wants to show everyone he can really swim."
5. Which animals in the book can fly?
   **A:** "The butterfly and the bird."
6. What can YOU do? 
   **A:** "I can ... and ... ." (自由回答)
