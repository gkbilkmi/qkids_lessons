# 《All About Me》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. What do you think this story is about?"
**A:** "I think it's about a boy's body parts — hands, ears, eyes, nose. / A fun song about me."

### (2/8) `clap, clap`

#### 💬
1. "What does the boy have? What sound does it make?"
   **A:** "He has two hands. Clap, clap."
2. "How many hands do you have? Show me!"
   **A:** "I have two hands. / Here they are."
3. "What can you do with your hands?"
   **A:** "I can clap / wave / draw / pick up things."

### (3/8) `tap, tap`

#### 💬
1. "What does the boy have here? What do they do?"
   **A:** "He has two elbows. Tap, tap."
2. "Why do you think the boy crosses his arms? (推断性)"
   **A:** "Because he wants to show his elbows and tap them together."
3. "Do you tap your elbows on the table at home? When?"
   **A:** "Sometimes, when I play at the table. / No, I don't."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `I have two hands`：我有两只手
2. `clap, clap`：拍一拍（拍手声）
3. `tap, tap`：轻敲两下
4. `wiggle, wiggle`：扭一扭、动一动
5. `blink, blink`：眨一眨
6. `knock, knock`：敲一敲
7. `click, click`：咔哒咔哒
8. `only one nose`：只有一个鼻子

### 二、语法知识点
1. `I have two + 复数身体部位`：表示"我有两个……"，hands/elbows/ears/eyes/knees/feet 都是复数，其中 foot 的复数是 feet（不规则变化）。
2. `But I have only one nose`：`but` 表示转折，`only` 意为"只、仅仅"，强调数量少。
3. 拟声叠词 clap-clap、tap-tap、wiggle-wiggle 等用"动词, 动词"的重复形式，读起来有节奏，适合边念边做动作。

### 三、故事内容提问
1. What body parts does the boy show?
   **A:** "His hands, elbows, ears, eyes, knees, feet, and nose."
2. How many hands, ears, and eyes does the boy have?
   **A:** "Two hands, two ears, and two eyes."
3. How many noses does he have?
   **A:** "Only one nose."
4. What do we do with our eyes and ears?
   **A:** "We blink our eyes and wiggle our ears."
5. Is this story a song? How do you know?
   **A:** "Yes, the same words come back again and again like a song."
