# 《Living in the Tree》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. There's a little owl on a branch, and a spider is sitting on a web in the tree. What do you think this story is about?"
**A:** "It's about the animals that live in a tree."

---

### (2/5) `owl` `spider`
#### 💬
1. "Who is asking the question on this page?"
**A:** "The owl."
2. "'Whooo is living in this old oak tree with me?' What does 'with me' tell us about the owl? Does he want company?"
**A:** "Yes, he wants company. Maybe he feels a little lonely in the big old tree."
3. "If you lived in a big old tree, which animal would you want as your neighbor? Why?"
**A:** "A squirrel / a bird, because it is cute and friendly."

### (3/5) `bee` `raccoon`
#### 💬
1. "What are the bees doing around the tree?"
**A:** "They are flying and making honey in the beehive."
2. "Where is the raccoon? What is it doing?"
**A:** "It is sitting up in the oak tree."
3. "The spider, the bee, and the raccoon all say the same thing. What is it?"
**A:** "'I am.'"
4. "Which of these animals have you seen in real life? Where did you see it?"
**A:** "I have seen bees / squirrels in the park near my home."

### (4/5) `caterpillar` `squirrel`
#### 💬
1. "What is the caterpillar doing on the tree?"
**A:** "It is climbing up the tree and eating leaves."
2. "Which animal here is the best climber? How can you tell?"
**A:** "The squirrel, because it can run up and down the tree very fast."
3. (推断性) "All the animals answer with the same two words — 'I am.' Why do you think the story repeats them again and again?"
**A:** "Because they all live in the tree, and the repetition makes the story sound like a song."
4. "Would you like to climb a big tree like the squirrel? Why / why not?"
**A:** "Yes, because it looks fun and I can see far. / No, because I am afraid of falling."

### (5/5) `until bedtime`
#### 💬
1. "Who says 'I am' at the end of the story?"
**A:** "The girl."
2. "Why does the girl say 'but only until bedtime'?"
**A:** "Because she doesn't really live in the tree — she plays there, but she goes home to sleep at night."
3. (推断性) "Who do you think loves the tree more — the animals or the girl? Why?"
**A:** "The animals, because it is their home. / The girl, because she can choose to visit and play there."
4. "The girl is sitting under the tree with her picnic. What do you like to do outside with your family?"
**A:** "I like to have a picnic / ride a bike / play ball in the park."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `live in` 住在……里
2. `old oak tree` 老橡树
3. `with me` 和我一起
4. `on a branch` 在树枝上
5. `until bedtime` 直到睡觉时间

### 二、核心句型
1. `Who is living in...?` 谁住在……？
2. `"I am," said the...` "是我，"……说（直接引语）
3. `What is the ... doing?` ……正在做什么？

### 三、语法知识点
1. 现在进行时
   - 例词：is living, is climbing, is flying, is eating
   - 例句：Who is living in this old oak tree with me? / The caterpillar is climbing the tree.
2. 方位介词 in / on
   - 例词：in the tree, on a branch, on a web
   - 例句：The owl is on a branch. / The spider is on a web in the tree.
3. 直接引语（引号内为角色原话）
   - 例句："Whooo?" asked the owl. / "I am," said the spider.

### 四、故事内容提问
1. Who asks the question first in the story?（故事里谁先提问？）
   **A:** The owl.
2. Who lives in the old oak tree?（谁住在这棵老橡树上？）
   **A:** A spider, a bee, a raccoon, a caterpillar, and a squirrel.
3. Why does the owl ask "Who is living in this old oak tree with me?"（猫头鹰为什么这么问？）
   **A:** Because he wants to know who his neighbors are — he wants company.
4. Why does the girl say "only until bedtime"?（女孩为什么说"只到睡觉时间"？）
   **A:** Because she plays in the tree, but she goes home to sleep at night.
5. What do all the animals say?（所有小动物都说了什么？）
   **A:** "I am."
