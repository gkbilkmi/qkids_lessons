# 《A Winter Home》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. We see a little chipmunk. What do you think 'a winter home' means? Where do you think he will sleep when it gets cold?"
**A:** "A winter home is a warm, safe place to sleep in the cold months. I think he will dig a hole or find a cozy spot."

---

### (2/6) `winter`
#### 💬
1. "What is the chipmunk looking for, and why does he need it in winter?"
**A:** "He is looking for a bed for winter, because it is cold and he needs a warm, safe place to sleep."
2. (推断性) "Why do you think animals need a special place to sleep in winter, but not in summer?"
**A:** "Because winter is cold and food is hard to find, so they need to stay safe and warm. In summer it is warm and they can move around."
3. "When does it get cold where you live? Where do you like to sleep when it's cold?"
**A:** "It gets cold in December. I like to sleep under a warm blanket."

---

### (3/6) `den` `pond`
#### 💬
1. "What two places does the chipmunk try first — a den and a pond? Are they good for winter?"
**A:** "He tries a den and a pond. They are not good places for a winter bed."
2. (推断性) "Why might a pond be a bad place to sleep in winter? Think about what happens to water when it gets cold."
**A:** "Because water gets very cold and can turn to ice, so it is not safe or warm."
3. "Have you ever seen a den or a pond? What animals live in a den?"
**A:** "Yes, I saw a pond. Bears and foxes live in a den."

### (4/6) `nest` `leaves`
#### 💬
1. "Can the chipmunk sleep in a nest or in the leaves? What does the book say?"
**A:** "No. The book says no, not in a nest, and the leaves are not a good bed for winter."
2. (推断性) "Why are leaves not a good winter bed? What happens to leaves when it gets cold?"
**A:** "Leaves blow away or get wet and cold, so they cannot keep him warm."
3. "Do you like playing in leaves? What do you like to do in autumn?"
**A:** "Yes, I like jumping in leaves. I like to collect leaves in autumn."

---

### (5/6) `safe`
#### 💬
1. "The chipmunk tries an old tree. Is it a good winter bed? Why or why not?"
**A:** "No. The book says an old tree is the spot for a summer nap, and it is not safe for winter."
2. (推断性) "What does 'safe' mean here? Why is the old tree not safe for winter?"
**A:** "Safe means protected from danger. The old tree is not safe because it is cold and open in winter."
3. "Where do you feel safe at home? What makes a place feel safe to you?"
**A:** "I feel safe in my bed. My family and the warm room make it safe."

---

### (6/6) `hole` *deep - warm*
#### 💬
1. "Where does the chipmunk finally go? What does he do?"
**A:** "He digs a hole in the ground. The hole is deep and warm."
2. "Is the hole a good winter home? Why?"
**A:** "Yes, it is perfect, because it is deep and warm and safe from the cold."
3. (推断性) "Why is underground a good place to sleep in winter? What other animals sleep underground?"
**A:** "Underground stays warm and safe. Rabbits and some frogs also sleep underground."
4. "If you were a little animal, what would your winter home look like?"
**A:** "I would have a small, warm hole with soft leaves."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `a bed for winter` 过冬的床／睡处
2. `sleep in a den / pond / nest` 睡在洞穴／池塘／鸟巢里（sleep in + 地点）
3. `not a good bed for the winter` 不是过冬的好睡处
4. `a summer nap` 夏日小睡（nap = 打盹）
5. `safe for winter` 过冬安全（safe + for + 用途）
6. `dig a hole in the ground` 在地里挖洞（dig + 宾语 + in + 地点）
7. `a perfect winter home` 完美的过冬之家

### 二、核心句型
1. `Can he + 动词原形 + 地点?` 他能（在某地）……吗？（Can he sleep in a den?）
2. `It is + adj. + for + 用途` ……对（某用途）来说是……的（It's not safe for winter.）
3. `No, not in a + 名词` 不，不在……里（省略式否定回答）

### 三、语法知识点
1. 一般现在时（三单）
   - 例词：is, looks, sleeps, digs
   - 例句：The chipmunk is looking for a bed. / He digs a hole in the ground.
2. 情态动词 can（表能力／可能性）
   - 例词：can
   - 例句：Can he sleep in a den? / Where can he go?
3. 形容词作表语
   - 例词：deep, warm, safe, perfect, good
   - 例句：The hole is deep and warm. / It is a perfect winter home.

### 四、故事内容提问
1. What is the chipmunk looking for?
   **A:** A bed for winter — a safe, warm place to sleep.
2. Which places does he try and reject?
   **A:** A den, a pond, a nest, the leaves, and an old tree.
3. Why is the old tree only good for a summer nap?
   **A:** Because it is not safe for winter; it is cold and open.
4. Where does he finally make his winter home?
   **A:** He digs a deep, warm hole in the ground — a perfect winter home.
5. Why is underground a good place to sleep in winter?
   **A:** Because it stays warm and safe from the cold.
