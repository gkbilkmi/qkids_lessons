# 《The New Baby》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. A big boy is touching a baby's hand. Who do you think this baby is?"
**A:** "Maybe the baby is his new little sister / brother."

---

### (2/9) `after school`
#### 💬
1. "Tom came home after school. What did he say to Poppa?"
**A:** "He said, 'Hello, Poppa.'"
2. (推断性) "Mum and Nana are not at home. Where do you think they are?"
**A:** "Maybe they went to the hospital / Maybe they went out."
3. "Do you have a grandma at home? What do you call her?"
**A:** "Yes, I have a grandma. I call her Grandma / Nana."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `come home` 回家 — "Tom came home after school."
2. `after school` 放学后
3. `at the hospital` 在医院 — "Mum is at the hospital."
4. `go to bed` 上床睡觉 — "Tom went to bed."
5. `go to sleep` 睡着 — "He went to sleep."
6. `wake up` 醒来 — "Poppa woke up."
7. `look after` 照顾 — "I'm going to help you look after Emma."

### 二、语法知识点
1. 一般过去时（规则变化 +ed）— "Tom came home after school." / "Poppa shouted..."
2. `be going to` 表将来 — "I'm going to help you look after Emma."
3. 一般现在时表喜好 — "I like cooking." / "I like little babies."
4. Where 引导的特殊疑问句 — "Where is Mum? Where is Nana?"

### 三、故事内容提问
1. Where was Mum at the beginning of the story?  **A:** "Mum was at the hospital."
2. Who stayed with Tom?  **A:** "Poppa stayed with Tom."
3. What did Poppa cook?  **A:** "Poppa cooked fish."
4. Who called at night? What was the news?  **A:** "The hospital called. Baby Emma was here."
5. How did Tom feel at the end?  **A:** "Tom felt happy. He liked little babies."
6. Why did Tom want to help with the baby?  **A:** "Because he loved his family and wanted to be a good helper."
