# 《Melting ice cubes》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. What do you see on the plate?"
**A:** "Colorful ice cubes."

---

### (2/9) `red` `ice cube`

#### 💬
1. "What color is this ice cube?"  **A:** "Red."
2. "Have you ever seen red ice at home? What did you think of it?"  **A:** "Yes, in the freezer / It looked cold and sweet."
3. "(推断性) Why do you think the ice cube is red instead of white or clear?"  **A:** "Someone put red juice or food coloring in it."

---

### (3/9) `melt` `liquid`

#### 💬
1. "What is happening to the red ice cube?"  **A:** "It is melting."
2. "What is the red stuff around the ice cube now?"  **A:** "It is red liquid / melted ice water."
3. "Why do ice cubes melt when they are not in the freezer?"  **A:** "Because the room is warm."

### (4/9) `yellow` `ice cube`

#### 💬

3. "Which color ice cube do you like best so far — red or yellow? Why?"  **A:** "Yellow, because it is bright / Red, because it looks like strawberry."

### (8/9) `mix` `together`

#### 💬

2. "Where do the red, yellow, and green water meet?"  **A:** "In the middle of the plate."
3. "(推断性) Why don't the colors stay separate? What happens when they mix?"  **A:** "Because water flows and pushes the colors together / They might make new colors."

### (9/9) `water` `color`

#### 💬
1. "What are we looking at now?"  **A:** "Colored water."
2. "What happened to the ice cubes?"  **A:** "They all melted into water."
3. "Why is the water not one single color?"  **A:** "Because each ice cube was a different color and they haven't fully mixed yet."
4. "Do you think all three colors will become one color if we wait longer? Why?"  **A:** "Yes, they will mix into one new color / No, maybe they stay separate."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配

1. `look at` 看……
2. `ice cube` 冰块
3. `turn into` 变成……

### 二、语法知识点

1. 现在进行时 — `is melting` / `are melting`（正在融化）
2. 一般现在时 vs 进行时 — `The ice cube melts.`（规律）vs `The ice cube is melting.`（正在发生）
3. 颜色形容词前置 — `a red ice cube` / `a yellow ice cube` / `a green ice cube`

### 三、故事内容提问

1. What colors are the ice cubes?  **A:** Red, yellow, and green.
2. What happens to the ice cubes over time?  **A:** They melt into colored water.
3. Why are there different colors of water on the plate?  **A:** Because each ice cube was a different color and they melted separately.
4. What is the story trying to tell us?  **A:** Ice melts in warm places; colors can mix together.
