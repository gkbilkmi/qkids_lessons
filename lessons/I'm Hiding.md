# 《I'm Hiding...》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. Where is the girl? Do you think she is playing a game?"
**A:** "She is behind the curtain. Yes, I think she is playing hide-and-seek."

---

### (2/6) `hiding` `behind` `curtain`
#### 💬
1. "Where is the girl hiding?"  **A:** "She is hiding behind the curtain."
2. "Why does she ask 'Am I hiding behind the curtain?' instead of just telling Papa where she is?"  **A:** "Because she is playing a guessing game and giving Papa clues."
3. "Where do you like to hide when you play hide-and-seek?"  **A:** "I like to hide behind the sofa / under the table."

### (3/6) `beside` `chair`
#### 💬
1. "Where does she look next?"  **A:** "She looks beside the chair."
2. "Is she really beside the chair? How do you know?"  **A:** "No, she isn't. She is still hiding and asking Papa a question."
3. "What toys can you see beside the chair? What toys are beside your chair at home?"  **A:** "I can see a teddy bear. My ball is beside my chair at home."

### (4/6) `under` `bed`
#### 💬
1. "Is the girl under the bed?"  **A:** "No, she isn't."
2. "Why does she say 'No, I'm not there'?"  **A:** "Because she is giving Papa clues and moving to a new hiding place."
3. "What do you have under your bed at home?"  **A:** "I have some boxes and shoes under my bed."

### (5/6) `inside` `basket` `sheet`
#### 💬
1. "Where does she hide at the end?"  **A:** "She hides inside the basket, under a sheet."
2. "How does Papa know where she is?"  **A:** "Her feet are sticking out, so he can see them."
3. "Where do you hide your toys when you clean your room?"  **A:** "I hide my toys inside a box / in the toy basket."

### (6/6) `tickle` `feet`
#### 💬
1. "What does Papa do when he finds her?"  **A:** "He tickles her feet."
2. "How does the girl feel at the end? Why?"  **A:** "She feels happy and surprised because Papa tickles her sticking-out feet."
3. "Do you like being tickled? Who tickles you at home?"  **A:** "Yes, I do. My dad tickles me at home."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `play hide-and-seek` 玩捉迷藏
2. `behind the curtain` 在窗帘后面
3. `beside the chair` 在椅子旁边
4. `under the bed` 在床下面
5. `inside the basket` 在篮子里面
6. `under a sheet` 在床单下面
7. `stick out` 伸出来
8. `tickle someone's feet` 挠某人的脚

### 二、语法知识点
1. 现在进行时 — 例：`I'm hiding.` / `Am I hiding behind the curtain?`
2. 方位介词 — `behind` / `beside` / `under` / `inside`
3. 一般疑问句的否定回答 — 例：`No, I'm not there.`

### 三、故事内容提问
1. What game is the girl playing with Papa?  **A:** "She is playing hide-and-seek."
2. Where does she hide first?  **A:** "She hides behind the curtain."
3. Is she under the bed?  **A:** "No, she isn't."
4. Where does she hide at the end?  **A:** "Inside the basket, under a sheet."
5. How does Papa find her?  **A:** "He sees her sticking-out feet."
6. How does the story end?  **A:** "Papa tickles her feet and they laugh together."
