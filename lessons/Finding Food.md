## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `want to eat` 想吃
2. `want to make` 想做（烹饪）
3. `find spoons in the kitchen` 在厨房里找到勺子
4. `make soup` 煮汤

### 二、语法知识点
1. 一般现在时 — 第三人称单数：`Mouse finds` / `Squirrel finds` / `They want`
2. 名词复数：`spoons` / `carrots` / `potatoes` / `onions`
3. 连词 `and` 连接两个主语：`Mouse and Squirrel are hungry.`

### 三、故事内容提问
1. What do Mouse and Squirrel feel at the start?  **A:** They are hungry.
2. What does Mouse find first?  **A:** A carrot.
3. What does Squirrel find?  **A:** A potato.
4. What do they want to make at the end?  **A:** Soup.
