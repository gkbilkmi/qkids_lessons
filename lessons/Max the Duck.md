## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `black vest`：黑色马甲/背心
2. `look at the clock`：看看钟
3. `be late`：迟到（I am late.）
4. `must run`：必须跑了（must + 动词原形）
5. `get dressed`：穿衣服（插图情境：妈妈帮 Max 换衣服）

### 二、语法知识点
1. `I am Max. / I am a duck. / I am late.` — "I am + 名词/形容词"自我介绍与状态描述。
2. `I have a black vest.` — have 表示"拥有"，颜色形容词放在名词前（a black vest）。
3. `I like my black vest.` — like + 名词，my 表示"我的"。
4. `Max must run.` — must 情态动词，后接动词原形，表示"必须"。

### 三、故事内容提问
1. What is Max's name and what does he have?
   **A:** "He is Max, and he has a black vest."
2. What does Max say about his vest?
   **A:** "'I like my black vest.'"
3. What does Mom say to Max?
   **A:** "'Look at the clock, Max.'"
4. (推断性) Why does Max have to run at the end?
   **A:** "Because he is late and the school bus is waiting."
5. What color is the bus?
   **A:** "Yellow."
