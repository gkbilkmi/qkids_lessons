# 《What Am I?》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. Can you see the ladybird? It is red and black. What do you think this book is about?"
**A:** "It is about little bugs / animals that can fly."

### (2/6) `bee`
#### 💬
1. "It is little. It is brown and yellow. What is it?"
**A:** "It is a bee."
2. "What do bees make?"
**A:** "Bees make honey."
3. "Do you like bees? Are you afraid of them?"
**A:** "Yes, I like bees. / No, I am a little afraid of bees."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `I am little.` 我很小。
2. `I like to fly.` 我喜欢飞。
3. `Look at me.` 看我。
4. `brown and yellow` 棕黄相间的
5. `red and black` 红黑相间的
6. `What am I?` 我是什么？

### 二、语法知识点
1. 颜色形容词 — brown（棕色）, yellow（黄色）, blue（蓝色）, red（红色）, black（黑色）
2. `I am...` 自我介绍句型 — "I am little." / "I am a bee!"
3. `I like to + 动词原形` — "I like to fly."
4. 不定冠词 a/an — a bee, a butterfly, a moth, a ladybird, a beetle
5. What 引导的特殊疑问句 — "What am I?"

### 三、故事内容提问
1. How many animals are in the book?  **A:** "There are five animals."
2. What colors is the bee?  **A:** "The bee is brown and yellow."
3. Which animal is blue?  **A:** "The butterfly is blue."
4. What has big eyes on its wings?  **A:** "The moth has big eyes on its wings."
5. Which animal is red and black?  **A:** "The ladybird is red and black."
6. What do all the animals like to do?  **A:** "They all like to fly."
