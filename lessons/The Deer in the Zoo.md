# 《The Deer in the Zoo》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. A little green jeep is driving into the trees, and there is a deer and a giraffe. Where do you think this family is going?"
**A:** "They are going to the zoo / to see the animals."

### (2/12) `zoo`
#### 💬
1. "Who is standing at the big green gate?"  **A:** "A dad, a girl, and two boys."
2. "Have you ever been to a zoo? What animals did you see there?"  **A:** "Yes, I saw monkeys and elephants / No, but I want to go."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配

1. `go to the zoo` 去动物园
2. `go in the jeep` 坐吉普车去
3. `feed the sheep / deer / shark` 喂绵羊 / 鹿 / 鲨鱼
4. `under a tree` 在树下
5. `go home` 回家
6. `fall asleep` 睡着

### 二、语法知识点

1. 一般现在时第一人称复数 — `We go…` / `We feed…` / `We can see…`（书中原句，讲一家人的行程）
2. 介词 in / under — `in the jeep`（在车里）vs `under a tree`（在树下）
3. 单复数同形 — `one sheep / two sheep`、`one deer / two deer`（不加 -s）；`eel → eels` 要加 -s
4. `too` 表示"也" — `My brother comes too.`（放句末）

### 三、故事内容提问

1. Who goes to the zoo?  **A:** A dad, a girl, two boys, and the little brother.
2. How do they travel in the zoo?  **A:** They go in a green jeep.
3. What animals do they feed?  **A:** They feed the sheep, the deer, the shark, and the eels.
4. What does the little brother do at the end?  **A:** He falls asleep on the way home.
5. What is the story trying to tell us?  **A:** A family can have a fun day together at the zoo, and it can make everyone very tired.
