# 《Cars》
#### 💬 "Look at the cover. What do you think this book is about?"
**A:** "It is about cars. / Different cars with different colors and shapes."

---

### (2/5) `small` `red`
#### 💬
1. "Look at the car. Is it small or big?"  **A:** "It is small."
2. "What color is the car?"  **A:** "It is red."
3. "Do you like small cars or big cars? Why?"  **A:** "I like big cars, because my family has a big car. / I like small cars, because they are cute."

### (4/5) `tall` `orange`
#### 💬
1. "Look at this car. Is it tall or short?"  **A:** "It is tall."
2. "Compare the orange car and the green car. Which one is taller?"  **A:** "The orange car is taller."
3. "(推断性) The dog keeps driving a different car. Why do you think he does that?"  **A:** "Because he wants to try new cars. / He likes cars with different colors and shapes."
4. "Who is the tallest person in your family?"  **A:** "My dad is the tallest. / My brother is the tallest."

#### 💬

3. "Why do you think a duck drives a banana car?"  **A:** "Because it is funny. / Because the car is yellow, like a banana."

## 📚 语言知识点
### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `look at me` 看我
2. `in a small red car` 在一辆红色的小汽车里
3. `banana yellow` 香蕉黄（像香蕉一样的黄色）
### 二、语法知识点
1. "Look at me, I am in a …" — 介绍自己所在的位置，I am + 地点
2. 多个形容词连用时，大小/形状在前，颜色在后：`a small red car`（不说 a red small car）
3. 比较级：`tall` → `taller`（Page 4 可用 "The orange car is taller." 操练）
### 三、故事内容提问
1. What is on the cover?  **A:** "A dog in a red car, and the word 'Cars'."
2. How many cars does the dog drive?  **A:** "Four cars. / Three cars and one banana car."
3. What color is the square car?  **A:** "It is green."
4. Which car is the tallest?  **A:** "The orange car."
5. Who drives the last car?  **A:** "A duck."
6. Which car do you like best? Why?  **A:** "I like the banana car, because it is funny and yellow."
