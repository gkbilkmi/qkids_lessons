## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `What do you have...?` 你有什么…？（询问拥有）
2. `in the bag` 在包里
3. `for my dad` / `for Ben` 给我爸爸 / 给 Ben（for 表"给、为了"）

### 二、语法知识点
1. 一般现在时表拥有：`I have a pet.`；口语中 `I got a ___`（= I've got，表示"我有 / 我得到"）。
2. 介词 `for` 表接收者：`for my dad`（给我爸爸）、`for me`（给我自己）、`for Ned`（给 Ned）。

### 三、故事内容提问
1. What does the child pull out of the bag on each page?  **A:** "A red cap, a pen, a pet, and a top."
2. Who receives a gift on page 3 and page 4?  **A:** "Dad gets the cap; Ben gets the pen."
3. What question is repeated through the whole book?  **A:** "'What do you have in the bag?'"
4. Why does the story end with 'for me' and 'for Ned'?  **A:** "The child keeps one gift and gives one to a friend — it's about sharing."
5. What would you put in your bag to show a friend?  **A:** "I would put a ___ in my bag."
