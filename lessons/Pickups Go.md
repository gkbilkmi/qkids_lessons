#### 💬 "Why would someone choose a pickup instead of a small car?"
**A:** "Because a pickup can carry big or heavy things, and it is useful for work on a farm or in a town."

**A:** "Yes, I see them near the market. People carry vegetables, boxes, and tools."

### (2/6) `empty`

#### 💬
1. "The little pickup is `empty`. What does that mean here?"  **A:** "It means there is nothing in the back of the pickup."
2. "(推断性) Why is an empty pickup useful?"  **A:** "Because it has space to load things, and it is lighter, so it may use less fuel."
3. "The cat is standing beside the pickup. Where do you think he is going?"  **A:** "Maybe to the farm stand to buy or load fruits and vegetables."
4. "When was the last time you saw something empty that is usually full?"  **A:** "I saw an empty bus after school / an empty box at home."

### (3/6) `full`

#### 💬
1. "This little pickup is `full`. What is in the back?"  **A:** "There are baskets of carrots, apples, broccoli, pumpkins, and green vegetables."
2. "How do you know it is full, and not just heavy?"  **A:** "The whole bed is packed with baskets, and there is no space left."
3. "(推断性) Why does the dog need a full pickup?"  **A:** "He is taking the food from the farm stand to a shop or a market."
4. "Which pickup is heavier — the empty one or the full one? Why?"  **A:** "The full one, because all the vegetables and baskets add a lot of weight."
5. "If you had a pickup, what would you fill it with?"  **A:** "I would fill it with books / sports equipment / fruit for my family."

### (4/6) `moving`

#### 💬
1. "This little pickup is `moving`. How can you tell?"  **A:** "There are speed lines behind it, and the wheels are on the road. The donkey is driving."
2. "Where might the donkey be going?"  **A:** "He may be going up the hill, to the town, or to deliver something."
3. "(推断性) There is a traffic light in the picture. Why is it important when a pickup is moving?"  **A:** "It tells drivers when to go and when to stop, so they do not have an accident."
4. "How do you travel to school or to a faraway place? Which vehicle do you use?"  **A:** "I go by car / bus / subway. For a faraway place, I take a plane or a train."

### (5/6) `still`

#### 💬
1. "`Still` is the opposite of `moving`. What is the pickup doing now?"  **A:** "It is stopped. It is not moving."
2. "Why is the sheep stopping?"  **A:** "There is a STOP sign, so she must stop and check the road."
3. "(推断性) Which is safer — a moving pickup or a still pickup? Why?"  **A:** "A still pickup is safer for people nearby, but a moving one is fine when the driver follows the road rules."
4. "What do you do while the car is still at a red light?"  **A:** "I wait, look out of the window, and stay in my seat."

### (6/6) `beep` `hill`

#### 💬
1. "What happens at the end of the story?"  **A:** "Many little pickups go beep, beep, beep all the way up the hill."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `pickup` / `pickup truck` 皮卡（车）
2. `be full of` 装满……
3. `go up the hill` 开上山坡
4. `traffic light` 红绿灯
5. `STOP sign` 停车标志
6. `farm stand` 路边农产品摊
7. `beep, beep, beep` 嘀嘀嘀（喇叭声）

### 二、语法知识点
1. **一般现在时 be 动词** — This little pickup **is** empty / full / moving / still. 单数主语用 is。
2. **this / these** — **This** little pickup is…（单数）→ **These** little pickups go…（复数）。
3. **形容词作表语** — `empty`（空的）、`full`（满的）、`moving`（移动的）、`still`（静止的）都放在 be 动词后面。
4. **现在分词作形容词** — moving 表示“正在移动的”，still 表示“不动的”，是一对反义表达。
5. **复数与动词** — these little pickup**s** go…；主语是复数，动词用原形 go。
6. **比较** — An empty pickup is **lighter** than a full one. A full pickup is **heavier**.

### 三、故事内容提问
1. What does the first little pickup look like?  **A:** "It is empty. A cat is standing next to it."
2. What makes the second pickup full?  **A:** "Baskets of carrots, apples, broccoli, pumpkins, and green vegetables."
3. Why does the green pickup stop?  **A:** "Because there is a STOP sign."
4. What do all the pickups do at the end?  **A:** "They go beep, beep, beep all the way up the hill."
5. What is the pattern in this book?  **A:** "It repeats 'This little pickup is…' with a new word on each page: empty, full, moving, still."
6. What is the book mainly about?  **A:** "Different little pickups doing different things on the road."
