#### 💬

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `a hot summer day` 炎热的夏日
2. `go to one's favorite shop` 去最喜欢的店
3. `ice cream` 冰淇淋
4. `one scoop of ice cream` 一勺冰淇淋
5. `a little more` 再来一点
6. `go off to the lake` 去湖边
7. `ice cream for all` 给大家的冰淇淋

### 二、语法知识点
1. 一般过去时 — 例：`It was a hot day.` / `She went off to the lake.` / `Lucy and Mary saw Doña Rosa.`
2. 礼貌请求 — 例：`I want some strawberry ice cream, please.` / `A little more, please.`
3. be 动词一般疑问句确认 — 例：`Is this enough?` / `Is this good?`

### 三、故事内容提问
1. Where does Doña Rosa go on the hot summer day?  **A:** "She goes to her favorite ice cream shop."
2. What does she ask the server for?  **A:** "She asks for strawberry ice cream."
3. How many times does she say "A little more, please"?  **A:** "Two times."
4. How many scoops does she get in the end?  **A:** "Three scoops."
5. Who does she meet at the lake?  **A:** "She meets Lucy and Mary."
6. What does "Ice cream for all!" mean?  **A:** "It means everyone can share the ice cream."
