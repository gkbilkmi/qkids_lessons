## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `skip and run` 蹦跳和奔跑
2. `jump in the leaves` 跳进落叶堆里
3. `in the sun` 在阳光下
4. `It is time to...` 是……的时候了
5. `go home` 回家
6. `go into our den` 走进我们的兽穴（家）
7. `tuck someone in bed` 帮某人盖好被子安顿睡下
8. `get set for...` 为……做好准备
9. `a long, long nap` 长长的一觉

### 二、语法知识点
1. 一般现在时 + be 动词表示正在发生 — `We are running.`（对比 `We run in the sun.` 表示常常做的事）
2. 主语 + 动词原形，第三人称单数加 -s — `My sister is running too.` / `Mom and Dad tuck us in bed.`
3. `too` 表示"也" — `My sister is running too.`
4. `I like to + 动词原形` — `I like to skip and run.` / `I like to jump in the leaves.`
5. `It's getting + 形容词` 表示情况正在变化 — `It's getting dark and cold outside.`
6. 祈使句与时间表达 — `It is time to go home.`

### 三、故事内容提问
1. Where does the bear family go to run?  **A:** They run in the sun in the autumn forest.
2. Who runs in the family?  **A:** Mom, dad and the little sister (and the little bear).
3. What does the little bear like to do?  **A:** He likes to skip and run, and he likes to jump in the leaves.
4. Why do they go into the den?  **A:** Because it is time to go home / it is getting late and cold.
5. What do Mom and Dad do when the bears are in bed?  **A:** They tuck them in bed.
6. What are the bears getting ready for?  **A:** They are getting set for a long, long nap.
