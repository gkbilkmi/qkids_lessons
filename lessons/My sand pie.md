# 《My sand pie》

#### 💬 封面预测题
"Look at the cover. What is the boy making in the sand? How do you know?"
**A:** "A sand pie — he is sitting in the sand with a plate and little leaves on top. The title says 'My sand pie'."

### (4/9) `sand`

#### 💬
1. "What is inside the bucket? What does sand feel like?"  **A:** "Sand. It feels soft, dry, and a little rough."
2. "What happens to sand when you add water? (推断性)"  **A:** "It gets wet and sticky, so it can hold its shape — better for a pie."
3. "Do you like playing in the sand? Where do you play?"  **A:** "Yes — at the beach / in a sand pit / in the park."

### (5/9) `water`

#### 💬
1. "What is the boy doing with the yellow jug?"  **A:** "He is pouring water onto the sand."
2. "Why do you need water for a sand pie? (推断性)"  **A:** "The water makes the sand wet and sticky, so the pie stays together."
3. "Which is better for building, wet or dry sand? Why?"  **A:** "Wet sand — it sticks and holds its shape."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `Here is the ___` 这是…（呈现实物）
2. `goes here` 放在这里
3. `sand pie` 沙子做的派

### 二、语法知识点
1. 一般现在时 + 方位副词 here：`Here is the sand.` / `The sand goes here.`
2. 名词复数的不规则变化：`leaf → leaves`（叶子，f 变 ves）。

### 三、故事内容提问
1. What does the boy need to make a sand pie?  **A:** "A bucket, a spade, sand, water, and a plate."
2. What goes in first — the sand or the water?  **A:** "The sand goes in first, then the water."
3. Why does the sand stay in the plate?  **A:** "Because he adds water, so the sand is wet and sticky."
4. What does he put on top at the end?  **A:** "Some leaves."
5. What does the boy say when he finishes?  **A:** "'Look at my sand pie.'"
6. Would you like to make a sand pie? What would you add?  **A:** "Yes — I would add a ___ / some leaves."
