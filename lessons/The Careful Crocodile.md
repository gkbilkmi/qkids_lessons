# 《The Careful Crocodile》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. What do you think this story is about?"
**A:** "I think it's about a mother crocodile sitting by her nest, taking care of her eggs."

---

### (2/5) `laid-40-eggs`

#### 💬
1. "Long, long ago, how many eggs did the old mother crocodile lay?"
   **A:** "She laid 40 eggs."

2. "Where did she lay the eggs?"
   **A:** "She laid them in a sandy nest by some ferns."

3. "Have you ever seen a nest with eggs in it? Whose nest was it?"
   **A:** "Yes, I saw a bird's nest in a tree. / No, I haven't seen one yet."

---

### (3/5) `watch-over-her-nest`

#### 💬
1. "Week after week, where did the mother crocodile stay? Why did she stay there?"
   **A:** "She stayed under the ferns, so she could watch over her nest."

2. "How do you think the mother felt while she watched the nest? How can you tell? (推断性)"
   **A:** "She felt a little tired, but careful and patient. She never left her eggs because she loves her babies."

3. "Who takes care of you when you sleep at night? What do they do for you?"
   **A:** "My mom takes care of me. She gives me a blanket and tells me a story. / My dad checks on me before he sleeps."

---

### (4/5) `ran-as-fast-as-she-could`

#### 💬
1. "One day, who found the nest? What did the mother crocodile do?"
   **A:** "A hungry dinosaur found the nest. The mother crocodile ran out of the ferns as fast as she could, and Snap! went her jaws."

2. "What does 'And that was the end of the egg-eating dinosaur!' mean? (推断性)"
   **A:** "It means the mother crocodile caught him and ate him, so he could not eat her eggs."

3. "When something scary happens, who keeps you safe? How do they keep you safe?"
   **A:** "My mom and dad keep me safe. They hold my hand when we cross the street. / My dad closes the door at night."

---

### (5/5) `coming-out-of-the-eggs`

#### 💬
1. "What did the mother hear the next day? What did she see in the nest?"
   **A:** "She heard some little cries from the nest. Baby crocodiles were coming out of the eggs."

2. "Why do you think the baby crocodiles cried? How did the mother feel? (推断性)"
   **A:** "They cried because they just came out of the eggs and wanted their mom. The mother felt very happy and proud."

3. "If you were a baby crocodile, what would you say to your mom when you first see her?"
   **A:** "Hello, Mom! I love you! / Mom, I'm hungry!"

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配

1. `long, long ago`：很久很久以前（讲故事的开头）
2. `in the days of the dinosaurs`：在恐龙时代
3. `lay eggs`：下蛋、产卵（书中过去式 `laid 40 eggs`）
4. `a sandy nest`：沙子做的巢
5. `by some ferns`：在蕨类植物旁边
6. `week after week`：一周又一周
7. `watch over her nest`：守护她的巢
8. `ran out of the ferns`：从蕨类植物里跑出来
9. `as fast as she could`：以她最快的速度
10. `Snap! Went her jaws.`：啪！她的嘴巴一咬
11. `that was the end of ...`：……就此完蛋了
12. `coming out of the eggs`：从蛋里钻出来

### 二、语法知识点

1. **一般过去时（不规则动词）**：故事里的动作都用过去式 —— `laid`（lay→laid）、`ran`（run→ran）、`found`（find→found）、`stayed`、`came`（come→came）。
2. **过去进行时**：`Baby crocodiles were coming out of the eggs.` —— were + V-ing 表示"正在往外钻"。
3. **`as ... as` 结构**：`as fast as she could` —— as + 形容词原级 + as，或接 `one can/could`，表示"尽可能……"。
4. **名词重复结构**：`week after week` 表示"接连不断地、一周又一周"，同类还有 `day after day`。
5. **拟声词倒装句**：`Snap! Went her jaws.` = `Her jaws went Snap!` 拟声词放在开头，句子更生动。
6. **合成形容词**：`egg-eating`（名词 + V-ing）意为"吃蛋的"，同类如 `meat-eating dinosaur` 食肉恐龙。

### 三、故事内容提问

1. When did the story happen?
   **A:** "It happened long, long ago, in the days of the dinosaurs."

2. How many eggs did the mother crocodile lay?
   **A:** "She laid 40 eggs."

3. What did the mother crocodile do week after week?
   **A:** "She stayed under the ferns and watched over her nest."

4. Why did the mother crocodile run out of the ferns?
   **A:** "Because a hungry dinosaur found the nest and wanted to eat her eggs."

5. What happened to the egg-eating dinosaur?
   **A:** "The mother crocodile went Snap! with her jaws, and that was the end of him."

6. What came out of the eggs the next day?
   **A:** "Baby crocodiles came out of the eggs."
