## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `look up high`：抬头看高处
2. `over the cars and trees`：越过汽车和树（over 表示“越过/在...上方”）
3. `fall down`：掉下来/落下来
4. `blow up a balloon`：吹气球
5. `just for me`：特地给我的

### 二、语法知识点
1. `My balloon is in the sky.` — be 动词 + 地点，in 表示"在...里"。
2. `My balloon is falling down.` — 现在进行时 (is + V-ing)，表示正在发生。
3. 拟声词和感叹词：Uh-oh!（糟糕啦）Pop!（砰！爆掉的声音）Yippee!（好耶！）
4. `Another balloon`：another + 单数名词，表示"又一个/再一个"。

### 三、故事内容提问
1. What does the girl say when her balloon flies into the sky?
   **A:** "Uh-oh! Uh-oh! Look up high. My balloon is in the sky!"
2. Where does the balloon go?
   **A:** "Over the cars and trees."
3. (推断性) What happens to the red balloon at the end?
   **A:** "It goes POP and falls down."
4. How does the girl feel on page 4 and on page 5? Why?
   **A:** "Sad first because her balloon pops; happy later because she gets a new balloon."
5. Who gives the girl a new balloon?
   **A:** "Mommy (blows it up) — and Daddy is with them."
6. What color is the new balloon?
   **A:** "Blue."
