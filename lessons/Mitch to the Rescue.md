### (4/9) `keep up with`

#### 💬
1. "What is the problem? Why is Ben worried?"
   **A:** "The last little duckling can't keep up with its mother. The water by the rocks is going too fast for it."
2. "What does 'keep up with' mean? Can a little duckling keep up with the others in fast water?"
   **A:** "It means to go as fast as someone else. No, it is too small and the water is too fast."
3. "(推断性) Why is fast water dangerous for a baby duckling?"
   **A:** "It may get swept away / It can't swim hard enough, so it can get lost or hurt."
4. "When do you have to keep up with someone? Like walking with Mom in a supermarket?"
   **A:** "I keep up with my mom / my friends, so I don't get lost."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `paddle a canoe`：划独木舟
2. `keep up with`：跟上（The last little duckling can't keep up with its mother.）
3. `scoop up`：一下子舀起/捧起（scoop up the duckling in his sunhat）
4. `get back into`：回到……里（get back into the canoe）
5. `paddle over to`：把船划到……（paddle over to the rocks）
6. `swim to`：游向（the duckling will swim to its mother）
7. `get wet`：弄湿、变湿（my new hat got wet）
8. `walk along the river bank`：沿着河岸走

### 二、语法知识点
1. 全文用一般过去时讲故事：said, paddled, climbed, got, walked — 动词过去式不规则变化（say→said, get→got）要重点记忆。
2. `be going to` 表示打算/将要："We are going to find your mother for you." — 计划好的将来动作。
3. `can't keep up with` 情态动词 can 否定 + 动词短语：_can't + 动词原形_ 表示"做不到"。
4. `but` 表转折："My new hat got wet, **but** I saved the duckling." — 前后对比，突出"救鸭子更重要"。
5. 多个动作连续就用 and 连接："Put your hat down in the water... **and** the duckling will swim to its mother."（祈使句 + and + 结果句）

### 三、故事内容提问
1. Who was in the canoe at the start of the story?
   **A:** Dad, Ben and Mitch.
2. What was the problem with the last little duckling?
   **A:** It couldn't keep up with its mother. The water by the rocks was too fast for it.
3. How did Mitch pick up the duckling?
   **A:** He scooped it up in his sunhat.
4. How did they find the mother duck?
   **A:** Ben saw the duck family under a big tree, and they paddled to the bank.
5. How did the duckling get back to its mother at the end?
   **A:** Mitch put his hat down in the water, and the duckling swam to its mother.
6. What is the best part of this story for you? Why?
   **A:** 开放题,如 "When the duckling swims to its mother — it's a happy ending."
