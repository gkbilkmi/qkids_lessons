## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `tummy` 肚子
2. `shoulders`
3. `gently`
4. *table - chair* （词族 / 比较级变化）

### 二、核心句型
1. `What do you + 动词?` 你……什么？
2. `Can you + 动词原形?` 你能……吗？
3. `I can + 动词原形` 我能……
4. `I have + 名词` 我有……

### 三、语法知识点
1. 现在进行时
   - 例词：touching, doing, playing, having, sleeping
   - 例句：Yes, maybe he is hungry / Maybe he is playing.
   - 例句：Yes, he is playing a patting game / He is having fun.
2. 一般现在时（三单）
   - 例词：is
   - 例句：Yes, he is patting the chair.
   - 例句：Yes, maybe he is hungry / Maybe he is playing.
3. 情态动词 can / could（表能力）
   - 例词：can, could
   - 例句：Yes, I can pat my tummy and my hair.
   - 例句：I can pat my desk / bed / book / toy.

### 四、故事内容提问
1. Why do you think the boy is patting his tummy? Is he hungry?
2. Why is the boy patting the table and chair? Is he playing a game?
3. Why does the book say 'gently, please'? Is the puppy sleeping?
