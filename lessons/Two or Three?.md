# 《Two or Three?》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. What do you think this story is about?"
**A:** "I think it's about two kids counting things at the beach — two and three."

#### 💬

### (3/5) `dig up` `two` `three` `shells`

#### 💬

3. "Who digs up more shells, Meg or Ed?"  **A:** "Meg — three is more than two."
4. "Have you ever found shells on the beach? What did you find?"  **A:** "Yes, I found a big shell / a white shell."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配

1. `on the sand` 在沙滩上
2. `dig up` 挖出来
3. `dig up two / three shells` 挖出两个 / 三个贝壳
4. `see two birds / three frogs` 看见两只鸟 / 三只青蛙

### 二、语法知识点

1. 情态动词 `can` — `Ed can see two birds.`（can + 动词原形，表示能力）
2. 数词与复数名词搭配 — `two shells` / `three birds` / `two frogs`（数词大于 1 时名词用复数）
3. 一般现在时 — `Meg and Ed are on the sand.`（be 动词 + 地点）
4. 选择疑问句式 — `Two or three?`（用 or 提供选择）

### 三、故事内容提问

1. Where are Meg and Ed?  **A:** On the sand at the beach.
2. What does Ed dig up? How many?  **A:** Two shells.
3. What does Meg dig up? How many?  **A:** Three shells.
4. What do the kids see in the sky?  **A:** Birds — Ed sees two, Meg sees three.
5. What do the kids see near the water?  **A:** Frogs — Ed sees two, Meg sees three.
6. (推断性) Who sees more things every time?  **A:** Meg.
