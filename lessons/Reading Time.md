## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `Here's your book.`：这是你的书 / 给你这本书
2. `sit on my lap`：坐在我的腿上
3. `have a look`：看一看
4. `point to`：指着……
5. `read to you`：读给你听
6. `wonderful words`：美妙的词语

### 二、语法知识点
1. "Here's your book." 用 Here 开头的倒装句，表示"给你……/这是……"，常用来递东西给孩子。
2. "Come sit on my lap and let's have a look." 祈使句省略主语 you；`let's = let us` 后接动词原形，表示"我们一起……"。
3. "You point to the bear..." 一般现在时描述书里反复发生的动作；`point to + 事物` 表示"指向某物"。
4. "I'll read to you the wonderful words." `I'll = I will` + 动词原形表示将要做的事；`read to sb.` 表示"读给某人听"。

### 三、故事内容提问
1. Who sits with the child on the armchair?
   **A:** "The dad."
2. What do the dad and the child do together?
   **A:** "They read a book together."
3. What does the child point to?
   **A:** "The bear, the truck, and the birds."
4. What will the dad read to the child?
   **A:** "The wonderful words."
5. How does the story end?
   **A:** "The dad reads the wonderful words to the child."
