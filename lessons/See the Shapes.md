# 《See the Shapes》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. The girl is pointing at a big blue circle. Can you name the shapes on the cover?"
**A:** "It is a circle. / I can see a circle."

---

### (2/9) `shapes`
#### 💬
1. "How many shapes can you see?"
**A:** "I can see six shapes."
2. (推断性) "Which shapes have no corners?"
**A:** "The circle and the oval have no corners."
3. "Which shape do you like best? Why?"
**A:** "I like the star. It is cool."

---

### (3/9) `circle`
#### 💬
1. "Can you see a circle? Where is it?"
**A:** "Yes, the basketball is a circle."
2. "What else is a circle?"
**A:** "A plate / the moon / a ball is a circle."
3. "Can you find a circle in your room? Show me."
**A:** "Yes, my clock / a plate is a circle."

---

### (4/9) `square`
#### 💬
1. "Can you see a square? What is it?"
**A:** "Yes, the door is a square."
2. (推断性) "Is the door a square or a rectangle? Why?"
**A:** "It is a square. Its four sides look the same."
3. "Can you find a square in your room? What is it?"
**A:** "My window / my book is a square."

---

### (5/9) `triangle`
#### 💬
1. "Can you see a triangle? Where is it?"
**A:** "Yes, the roof is a triangle."
2. "How many sides does a triangle have?"
**A:** "A triangle has three sides."
3. "Can you make a triangle with your fingers? Show me."
**A:** "Yes, I can. My fingers make a triangle."

---

### (6/9) `star`
#### 💬
1. "Can you see a star? Is it in the sky?"
**A:** "No, it is not in the sky. It is a starfish."
2. "How many points does this star have?"
**A:** "It has five points."
3. "Have you seen a starfish? Where?"
**A:** "Yes, I saw a starfish at the beach / in a book."

---

### (7/9) `oval`
#### 💬
1. "Can you see an oval? What is it?"
**A:** "Yes, the watermelon is an oval."
2. (推断性) "Is a circle the same as an oval? How are they different?"
**A:** "No. A circle is round. An oval is long and round."
3. "What food is an oval? Tell me."
**A:** "A watermelon / an egg / a mango is an oval."

---

### (8/9) `rectangle`
#### 💬
1. "Can you see a rectangle? Where is it?"
**A:** "Yes, the girl's card is a rectangle. The blocks are rectangles, too."
2. (推断性) "Is a square a rectangle? How do you know?"
**A:** "Yes. A square has four sides and four corners, too."
3. "What rectangle things are in your classroom?"
**A:** "The blackboard / the door / my book is a rectangle."

### (9/9) `See the shapes!`
#### 💬
1. "A star is like a starfish. What is a triangle like?"
**A:** "A triangle is like a roof."
2. "What is a circle like?"
**A:** "A circle is like a basketball."
3. "Look around you. Can you see a shape from this book?"
**A:** "Yes, my clock is a circle. / The screen is a rectangle."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `Look at the shapes.` 看这些形状。
2. `Can you see...?` 你能看到……吗？
3. `in your room` 在你的房间里
4. 实物名词 — basketball 篮球, door 门, roof 屋顶, starfish 海星, watermelon 西瓜, blocks 积木

### 二、语法知识点
1. 特殊疑问句 — "What shape is it?" / "How many sides does a triangle have?"
2. 一般疑问句 Can you...? — "Can you see a circle?"
3. 可数名词单复数 — one circle, two circles
4. 不定冠词 a/an — a circle, a square, a triangle, a star, an oval, a rectangle
5. `the same` 相同的 — "Its four sides look the same."

### 三、故事内容提问
1. How many shapes are in the book?  **A:** "There are six shapes."
2. What is a basketball like?  **A:** "A basketball is a circle."
3. What is a starfish like?  **A:** "A starfish is a star."
4. Is a square a rectangle?  **A:** "Yes, a square is a special rectangle."
5. Which shapes have no corners?  **A:** "The circle and the oval have no corners."
6. What is a roof like?  **A:** "A roof is a triangle."
