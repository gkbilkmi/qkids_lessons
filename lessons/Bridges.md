# 《Bridges》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. Can you see the bridge? It goes round and round. Have you ever crossed a bridge like this?"
**A:** "Yes, I have. / No, I haven't. It looks fun!"

---

### (2/9) `thousands of years`
#### 💬
1. "How long have people been making bridges?"
**A:** "People have been making bridges for thousands of years."
2. "Which bridge is very old? Which is very new?"
**A:** "The stone bridge is very old. The red bridge is very new."
3. "Do you like old bridges or new bridges? Why?"
**A:** "I like new bridges. They are big and colorful."

---

### (3/9) `cross over`
#### 💬
1. "What do bridges help people do?"
**A:** "Bridges help people cross over water and land."
2. (推断性) "What can't people do if there is no bridge over the river?"
**A:** "They can't cross the river. They need a bridge."
3. "Is there a bridge near your home? How do you cross it?"
**A:** "Yes, there is a big bridge near my home. I cross it by bus / by car."

### (4/9) `safe and strong`
#### 💬
1. "What is on this bridge?"
**A:** "There are a lot of cars, buses and people on this bridge."
2. "Why must bridges be very strong?"
**A:** "They must be strong to support all the cars, buses and people."
3. (推断性) "What may happen if a bridge is not strong?"
**A:** "It may break or fall down. It is not safe."

### (5/9) `arch - beam - steel ropes`
#### 💬
1. "How many good ways are there to make bridges safe and strong?"
**A:** "There are three good ways."
2. "The suspension bridge has big steel ropes. What do the ropes do?"
**A:** "The steel ropes support the bridge."
3. "Which bridge do you like best? Why?"
**A:** "I like the arch bridge. It looks like a rainbow."

#### 💬

**A:** "Because arches are very strong. They can last a long time."
3. "Have you seen an arch bridge? Where?"
**A:** "Yes, I saw an arch bridge in the park / in my city."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `thousands of years` 数千年
2. `cross over` 跨过 — "Bridges help people cross over water."
3. `a lot of` 许多 — "There are a lot of cars, buses and people on this bridge."
4. `make bridges` 造桥 — "People have been making bridges for thousands of years."
5. `steel ropes` 钢索
6. `safe and strong` 安全又坚固

### 二、语法知识点
1. 现在完成进行时 — "People have been making bridges for thousands of years."
2. 情态动词 must — "Bridges must be safe." / "They must be very strong..."
3. There be 句型 — "There are a lot of cars, buses and people on this bridge."
4. 动词不定式表目的 — "...to support all the cars, buses and people."
5. have to — "The steel ropes have to be big and strong to make the bridge safe."

### 三、故事内容提问
1. How long have people been making bridges?  **A:** "For thousands of years."
2. What do bridges help people do?  **A:** "They help people cross over water and land."
3. How many ways are there to make safe and strong bridges?  **A:** "Three ways — with arches, beams and steel ropes."
4. What supports a suspension bridge?  **A:** "Big steel ropes support it."
5. Why must bridges be very strong?  **A:** "To support all the cars, buses and people."
6. Which bridge is very old in the book?  **A:** "The old stone bridge with arches is very old."
