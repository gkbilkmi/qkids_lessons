### (2/7) `take care of`

#### 💬
1. "What pet does this boy have? What does he do every day?"
   **A:** "He has a pet turtle. He takes care of his turtle every day."
2. "Look at the boy and the turtle — they are face to face. (推断性) Do you think the turtle knows the boy?"
   **A:** "Yes — it is not hiding in its shell, so it feels safe with him." (合理即可)
3. "How do you take care of a turtle?"
   **A:** "Give it food and water, clean its home, and be gentle with it."
4. "Would you like a slow pet like a turtle? Why?"
   **A:** "Yes, because it is quiet / No, because it walks too slowly." (自由回答)

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `have a pet`：养宠物
2. `take care of`：照顾、照料
3. `every day`：每天
4. `a pet turtle / fish / bird / cat / dog`：宠物龟/鱼/鸟/猫/狗
5. `up close`：近距离地（妹妹凑近看鱼）
6. `walk a dog`：遛狗（插画情境：牵着狗绳跑）

### 二、语法知识点
1. `I have a pet + 单数名词.` — have 表示"拥有"，a + 单数可数名词。
2. `I take care of my turtle.` — my 表示"我的"；一般现在时，every day 表示习惯。
3. `We all have pets. We take care of our pets.` — 主语 We，my 变为 our；all 表示"都"。
4. 五页句子结构完全相同，只有中心词（turtle→fish→bird→cat→dog）变化，适合做替换操练。

### 三、故事内容提问
1. How many kinds of pets are in this book?
   **A:** "Five — a turtle, a fish, a bird, a cat, and a dog (and the rabbit on the last page)."
2. What does every child do every day?
   **A:** "They take care of their pets."
3. Which pet swims in water?
   **A:** "The fish."
4. Which pet has a hard shell?
   **A:** "The turtle."
5. (推断性) Why do pets need our care every day?
   **A:** "Because they need food, water, and love — just like us."
6. What do all the children say together at the end?
   **A:** "'We all have pets. We take care of our pets every day.'"
