# 《Hop, Hop》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. What do you think this story is about?"
**A:** "I think it's about two rabbits hopping and playing together outside."

---

### (2/5) `hop` `hill`

#### 💬
1. "Who is hopping up the hill?"  **A:** "The rabbit."
2. "Where is the little rabbit?"  **A:** "It is at the bottom of the hill with the bird."
3. "Can you hop like a rabbit? Let's try!"  **A:** "Yes, hop, hop, hop!"
4. "Have you climbed up a hill? Was it easy or hard?"  **A:** "Yes, it was a little hard / fun."

### (3/5) `up` `down`

#### 💬
1. "What is the rabbit doing now?"  **A:** "It is hopping down the hill."
2. "First it hopped up, now it hops...?"  **A:** "Down."
3. "(推断性) Why did the rabbit hop back down? What does it want to do?"  **A:** "It wants to play with the little rabbit / show it can hop."
4. "Do you like going up or coming down a slide better? Why?"  **A:** "Going up is slow, coming down is fast and fun / both!"

### (4/5) `puddle`

#### 💬
1. "What does the little rabbit say?"  **A:** "I can hop over this puddle."
2. "What is a puddle?"  **A:** "A little pool of water on the ground."
3. "What is the big rabbit doing?"  **A:** "It is watching the little rabbit."
4. "Do you like jumping over puddles after rain? Have you tried?"  **A:** "Yes, it's fun / No, I don't want to get wet."

### (5/5) `oops` `next time`

#### 💬
1. "Did the little rabbit hop over the puddle?"  **A:** "No, it fell in the puddle."
2. "What does the big rabbit say?"  **A:** "Oops, maybe next time."
3. "(推断性) How does the little rabbit feel now? How can you tell?"  **A:** "Maybe a little sad, but it is smiling — it's okay / it will try again."
4. "When you try something hard and it doesn't work, what can you say?"  **A:** "Oops, maybe next time! / I can try again."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配

1. `hop up the hill` 跳上山丘
2. `hop down the hill` 跳下山丘
3. `hop over...` 跳过……（hop over this puddle 跳过这个水坑）
4. `next time` 下次
5. `Oops!` 哎呀！（不小心出错时的感叹词）

### 二、语法知识点

1. 情态动词 `can` — `I can hop up the hill.`（can + 动词原形，表示能力）
2. 方位副词 `up / down / over` — `up the hill` / `down the hill` / `over this puddle`（表示上、下、越过）
3. `maybe` 表可能 — `Maybe next time.`（语气委婉，安慰别人常用）
4. 拟声重复 — `Hop, hop, hop.`（重复表现动作和节奏）

### 三、故事内容提问

1. What can the rabbit do at the start?  **A:** It can hop up the hill.
2. What does the rabbit do next?  **A:** It hops down the hill.
3. What does the little rabbit want to hop over?  **A:** The puddle.
4. What happens to the little rabbit?  **A:** It falls in the puddle.
5. What does the big rabbit say at the end?  **A:** "Oops, maybe next time."
6. (推断性) What will the little rabbit do next time?  **A:** It will try hopping over the puddle again.
