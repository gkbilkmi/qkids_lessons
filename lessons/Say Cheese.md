# 《Say Cheese》


#### 💬 "Look at the cover — two kids are holding cheese and smiling! In English we say 'Say cheese!' right before a picture. What do you say in Chinese before a photo?"
**A:** "We say '茄子' (qiezi)!"

### (2/9) `be made from`
#### 💬
1. _[生活关联]_ "Our title is 'Made From Milk.' So cheese comes from milk! What else in your kitchen is made from milk?"
**A:** "Yogurt, ice cream, butter, and milk tea — they're all made from milk."
1. (推断性) _[批判思考]_ "All cheese starts as milk — so why is some cheese soft, and some hard and crumbly?"
**A:** "Because they use different milk and make it in different ways."

### (3/9) `cows' milk - goats' milk - yaks' milk`
#### 💬
1. _[画面观察]_ "Look at this page. What animals do you see? Which ones give us milk?"
**A:** "I see cows, goats, sheep, buffaloes, camels, and yaks. I've seen cows, sheep, and goats, but only yaks in pictures."
1. _[文化对比]_ "Out in Inner Mongolia and Tibet, people make cheese from yak milk 牦牛奶. They turn it into 奶豆腐 and 酥油. Have you ever tasted any milk food from there?"
**A:** "I've tried yogurt and milk tea, but not 奶豆腐 yet. It looks a little like cheese."
1. (推断性) _[批判思考]_ "Why do you think different places use different animals' milk?"
**A:** "Because different animals live in different places — people just use what they have."

### (4/9) `From Milk to Cheese`
#### 💬
1. _[细节观察]_ "This page says cheese was made on farms 农场 for thousands of years. But today, most cheese comes from big factories 工厂. What do you think changed?"
**A:** "Machines in factories can make much more cheese, much faster, than one person by hand on a farm."
1. (推断性) _[批判思考]_ "Would factory cheese taste the same as farm cheese? Why?"
**A:** "Maybe not — farm cheese is made slowly by hand in small amounts, so it might taste different."
1. _[生活关联]_ "Think of a food in China that families used to make by hand at home — but now we buy it from a factory."
**A:** "Like dumplings, noodles, and tofu. But at Spring Festival 春节, my family still makes dumplings by hand."

### (5/9) `vats`
#### 💬
1. _[画面观察]_ "We're inside the factory now. What's happening to the milk? Where is it going?"
**A:** "The milk is poured into huge tubs called vats 大桶, then heated 加热 and mixed with special ingredients 配料."
1. _[画面观察]_ "Why do you think they heat it first?"
**A:** "To kill germs and help the milk start to change into cheese."
1. (推断性) _[假设提问]_ "What if they didn't heat the milk? What do you think would happen?"
**A:** "It might go bad, or it wouldn't turn into cheese properly."

---

### (6/9) `curds - whey`
#### 💬
1. _[词义推断]_ "After the milk is heated, look closely — what two parts do you see? Which one do you think becomes the cheese?"
**A:** "A thick lumpy part (curds 凝乳) and a watery part (whey 乳清). The curds become the cheese."
1. _[生活关联]_ "Curds are soft and a little lumpy. Have you eaten anything like that? Like 豆腐花 (tofu pudding), or yogurt with fruit?"
**A:** "I've had 豆腐花 — it's soft and smooth, a little like curds."
1. (推断性) _[批判思考]_ "So the factory drains off the whey 乳清 and throws it away. Can you think of a better use for it?"
**A:** "Maybe give it to animals, or use it in other food, so nothing is wasted."

### (7/9) `mature - fresh`
#### 💬
1. _[比较提问]_ "Some cheese sits in a cool room for weeks to get strong. Some is 'fresh' — we eat it right away. Which would you rather taste — the mild fresh one, or the strong, aged one?"
**A:** "I'd rather try the fresh one, because the mature cheese smells very strong."

#### 💬

**A:** "I need a lot of energy in PE class and when I play basketball."
1. _[生活关联]_ "Do you like cheese? What's your favourite way to eat it?"
**A:** "Yes! I like cheese on pizza and in 芝士奶盖 (cheese-topped milk tea)."

### (9/9) `Glossary`
#### 💬
1. _[词义推断]_ "The glossary 词汇表 at the back explains the tricky words. Which word matters most for making cheese — 'curds', 'whey', or 'mature'? Why?"

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `be made from` 由……制成（成品已看不出原样，与 `be made of` 区分）
2. `cows' milk / goats' milk / yaks' milk` 牛奶 / 羊奶 / 牦牛奶（复数名词所有格表来源）
3. `be poured into` 被倒入……
4. `drain off` 排掉；沥干
5. `grow strong` 变得强壮（grow + 形容词）
6. `right away` 立刻；马上
7. `by hand` 用手工；手工地

### 二、核心句型
1. `be made from ...` ……由……制成
2. `has been made ... for thousands of years` 已制作了几千年（现在完成时 + 被动）
3. `It helps sth. + 动词原形` / `It gives sb. sth.` 它帮助……做某事 / 它给……某物
4. `Some ... are ..., some ... are ...` 有些……是……，有些……是……

### 三、语法知识点
1. 被动语态（be + 过去分词）—— 本课主线
   - 例词：is made, is poured, is heated, is drained, are added, are kept, are put, can be eaten
   - 例句：All cheese is made from milk. / The milk is heated. / The whey is drained off.
1. 现在完成时的被动（has/have been + 过去分词）
   - 例词：has been made
   - 例句：Cheese has been made on farms for thousands of years.
1. 名词所有格表来源
   - 例词：cows', goats', sheep milk, yaks', buffaloes', camels'
   - 例句：Most cheese is made from cows' milk.

### 四、故事内容提问
1. What is all cheese made from?（所有奶酪是用什么做的？）
   **A:** Milk.
1. Which animals' milk can cheese be made from?
   **A:** Cows, goats, sheep, water buffaloes, camels, and yaks.
1. Why is cheese good for us?
   **A:** It helps our bones grow strong and gives us energy.
