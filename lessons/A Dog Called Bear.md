### (2/8) `belonged to`

#### 💬
1. "Who called out 'Matthew! Bear's here!'? What kind of dog is Bear?"
   **A:** "Henry. Bear is a big, brown dog — and he belonged to Matthew."
2. (推断性) "Bear followed Matthew all the way to school. Why do you think he did that?"
   **A:** "Because he loves Matthew / he didn't want to stay home alone." (合理即可)
3. "Do you have a pet, or would you like one? Who would it belong to?"
   **A:** "Yes, I have a .... / I would like a .... It belongs to ...." (自由回答)

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `belong to`：属于（He belonged to Matthew. 这只狗是 Matthew 的）
2. `take you home`：带你回家
3. `hurry up`：赶快
4. `wag his tail`：摇尾巴
5. `shut the gate`：关上栅栏门
6. `a little while later`：过了一小会儿
7. `come back`：回来
8. `a squeal of brakes`：刺耳的刹车声
9. `rush to the window`：冲向窗户
10. `stagger to his feet`：摇摇晃晃地站起来
11. `stop ... from ...`：阻止……做……（He stopped my little girl from running out on to the road.）
12. `a little bump`：轻轻撞了一下

### 二、语法知识点
1. 全文过去式叙事：called, was, belonged, wagged, didn't like, shut, heard, rushed, cry/cried, staggered。
2. `Bear's been hit!` — has been 被动完成式，表示"被撞了"。
3. `I'm going to have to take you home.` — be going to 表示即将要做的事。
4. `stop sb from doing sth`：阻止某人做某事，from 后接 V-ing（from running out）。
5. `didn't like hurrying`：like 后接 V-ing 表示喜欢做的事。

### 三、故事内容提问
1. Who did Bear belong to?
   **A:** "Matthew."
2. Why didn't Bear hurry?
   **A:** "Because he was an old dog and he didn't like hurrying."
3. What did everyone hear suddenly, and what did they do?
   **A:** "A squeal of brakes. Everyone rushed to the window."
4. What did Bear do when he saw the little girl near the road?
   **A:** "He stopped her from running out on to the road."
5. (推断性) Why is Bear a hero at the end?
   **A:** "Because he saved the little girl, even though he is old and slow."
6. How do we know Bear was still happy to see Matthew after the bump?
   **A:** "He wagged his tail hard when he saw Matthew."
