# 《How Elephants Lost Their Wings》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. What do you think this story is about?"

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `once upon a time`：从前、很久很久以前
2. `could fly`：能够飞（情态动词 could 表示过去的能力）
3. `flew everywhere`：到处飞
4. `crowed like roosters`：像公鸡一样打鸣
5. `shook below them`：在它们下方晃动
6. `flew into trees`：飞进了树里
7. `smashed them`：把它们撞坏了
8. `had to`/`must`：不得不、必须
9. `a grand feast`：一场盛大的宴席
10. `ate and ate`：吃了又吃
11. `when their tummies were full`：当它们的肚子填满时
12. `took away`：拿走
13. `got ... back`：把……拿回来
14. `never flew again`：再也没有飞过

### 二、语法知识点
1. 过去时叙事：故事以 `Once upon a time` 开头，全篇用一般过去时讲述（`could fly`、`flew`、`were noisy`、`yelled`、`took away`、`never flew again`）。
2. `but` 表示转折：本页用 `But they didn't get their wings back` 把前面的生气与后面的结果连起来。
3. `when` 引导时间状语从句：`When their tummies were full, they slept.`（当他们吃饱时，他们睡着了。）
4. `and` 连接并列动作：`They shouted and they stomped.`、`They yelled, and crowed like roosters.`
5. 情态动词 `must` 表示必须：`We must stop them.`
6. 感叹与拟人：`said the god` 把动物的故事讲成神话式的旁白。

### 三、故事内容提问
1. What could the elephants do at the beginning of the story?
   **A:** "They could fly with their wings."
2. Why were the elephants noisy?
   **A:** "Because they were flying and they yelled and crowed like roosters."
3. What happened to the trees and houses?
   **A:** "They shook, and then the elephants flew into the trees and smashed them."
4. What did the gods do to stop the elephants?
   **A:** "They invited them to a grand feast, and when the elephants slept, they took away their wings."
5. Who got the elephants' wings?
   **A:** "The peacocks got the wings."
6. Why did the elephants never fly again?
   **A:** "Because they never got their wings back."
