# 《Red, the Hen》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. This book is called 'Red, the Hen'. What do you think this story is about?"
**A:** "I think it's about a hen named Red." / "I think it's about a hen who lays eggs."

---

### (2/5) `hen`

#### 💬
1. "What is Red?"
**A:** "Red is a hen. She is a very good hen."
2. "What color is Red? Do you know any animal called Red?"
**A:** "She is red / red-brown." / "My uncle's chicken is called Red."
3. "(推断性) Why do you think the book tells us Red's name at the very beginning?"
**A:** "Because she is the main character in the story." / "So we can call her Red."

### (3/5) `egg`

#### 💬
1. "What does Red do every day?"
**A:** "She lays an egg a day. We get an egg a day from Red."
2. "Where does Red sit when she lays her eggs?"
**A:** "She sits on her nest in the hen house."
3. "Do you have a pet? Does it give you something every day?"
**A:** "Yes, I have a cat/dog. It gives me company every day." / "I get milk from the cow every day."

### (4/5) `pig pen`

#### 💬
1. "Where can we find an egg?"
**A:** "We can find an egg in the pig pen."
2. "Where else can we find an egg?"
**A:** "We can find an egg on the bed."
3. "(推断性) Why does Red hide her eggs in the pig pen and on the bed?"
**A:** "She wants to keep her eggs safe, so nobody can take them." / "She likes to hide them in funny places."
4. "Do you like to hide your toys? Where do you hide them?"
**A:** "Yes, I hide my toys under the bed." / "I hide my car behind the sofa."

### (5/5) `ten eggs`

#### 💬

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `a very good hen` 一只很棒的母鸡
2. `an egg a day` 一天一个蛋
3. `get an egg from Red` 从红母鸡那里得到一个蛋
4. `in the pig pen` 在猪圈里
5. `on the bed` 在床上
6. `in a net` 在网里
7. `find ten eggs` 找到十个蛋
8. `lay an egg` 下蛋
9. `sit on the nest` 坐在窝上

### 二、核心句型
1. `Red is a hen.` 红是一只母鸡。
2. `We get an egg a day from Red.` 我们每天从红那里得到一个蛋。
3. `We can find an egg in the pig pen.` 我们可以在猪圈里找到一个蛋。
4. `Can you find ten eggs from Red?` 你能找出红下的十个蛋吗？

### 三、语法知识点
1. `be` 动词与主语一致
   - 例句：Red is a hen. / She is a very good hen.
2. `can + 动词原形` 表示"能、会"，否定用 `can't`
   - 例句：We can find an egg. / Can you find ten eggs?
3. 一般现在时第三人称单数（he/she/it + 动词-s）
   - 例词：lays, gets, is
   - 例句：She lays an egg a day.
4. 不定冠词 a / an
   - `a` 用在辅音音素前：a hen, a day, a net
   - `an` 用在元音音素前：an egg, an egg a day
