## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配

1. `same as always` 和往常一样
2. `get ready` 准备好
3. `put on costumes` 穿上服装
4. `climb onstage` 爬上舞台
5. `peek through the curtains` 从帘子后面偷看
6. `everything is different` 一切都不一样了

### 二、语法知识点

1. 一般现在时 — `Mom walks me to school.` / `Eddie rides the school bus.`（日常习惯）
2. 现在进行时 — `We put on costumes and climb onstage.` / `The curtain opens.`（正在发生）
3. 转折词 `But` — `But then our teacher says...`（表示事情突然变化）
4. 强调词 `Even` — `Even Eddie's bus driver!`（表示出乎意料）

### 三、故事内容提问

1. How do the children get to school?  **A:** Mom walks the narrator, Leah's dad drives her, Eddie rides the school bus.
2. What do the children do during the school day?  **A:** They sing songs, build with blocks, and listen to a story.
3. Why does the day become different?  **A:** The teacher tells them to get ready, they put on animal costumes and put on a show.
4. What is the story trying to tell us?  **A:** Even an ordinary day can become special when something unexpected and fun happens.
