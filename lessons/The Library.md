## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配

1. `look, look` 看，看！（引起注意）
2. `so many` 如此多的
3. `say hello` 打招呼
4. `at the library` 在图书馆

### 二、语法知识点

1. 简单形容词 — big, small, fat, slim, blue, red, green（修饰名词时前置）
2. 反义词对比 — big ↔ small, fat ↔ slim
3. 现在进行时 — `They are waving.` / `They are going into the library.`（正在发生）
4. 感叹句 — `So many books!`（so + many + 名词复数）

### 三、故事内容提问

1. What do the children see at the library?  **A:** Many different books — big, small, fat, slim, blue, red, green.
2. What pattern does the book use to introduce each book?  **A:** "Look, look! It's a [adjective] book."
3. How do the children feel about the library?  **A:** They are excited and amazed — they end with 'So many books!'
