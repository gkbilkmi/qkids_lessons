# 《The Summer Camp》

#### 💬 "Look at the cover. You can see a fox, a rabbit, and a little chick — they all look happy. Where do you think they are going together?"
**A:** "They are going to summer camp together."

---

### (2/5) `counselor` `introduce ourselves`
#### 💬
1. "Lola Chick is excited because it is her first time at camp. How do you feel on the first day of something new — a new class, a new school, or a camp?"
**A:** "I feel a little nervous but also excited. After I meet new friends, I feel better and more comfortable."
2. "The counselor says, 'We're going to start with a song to introduce ourselves.' Is singing a good way to introduce yourself? How would you introduce yourself to new campers?"
**A:** "Yes, it is fun and helps everyone relax. I would say my name, my age, and what I like to do."
3. "Leo says 'I like singing too.' What does 'too' tell us about Leo and Lola?"
**A:** "It tells us they like the same thing. Having something in common helps them become friends."

---

### (3/5) `arts-and-crafts` `easel`
#### 💬
1. "At the arts-and-crafts area, the counselor asks, 'Who likes to paint?' Do you like to paint? What do you usually like to paint?"
**A:** "Yes, I like to paint. I usually paint animals and flowers, and I like to choose the colors myself."
2. "Lola found an easel and began painting. If you were at this camp, what would you paint? Why?"
**A:** "I would paint my family / my pet / a big tree house, because I love them / I think it is cool."
3. (推断性) "The campers keep doing things side by side — singing together, painting next to each other. Why do you think camp activities are like this? What does it tell us about camp?"
**A:** "Because camp is about doing things together and making friends, not just finishing an activity alone."

---

### (4/5) `make a snack` `fruit salad`
#### 💬
1. "'I like to eat,' said Lola Chick. What kind of food do you like to eat? Have you ever made a snack or a meal yourself?"
**A:** "I like noodles / fruit / rice. Yes, I have made eggs / a sandwich with my mom, and it was fun."
2. "A few of the campers made a fruit salad together. Why is cooking together a good camp activity?"
**A:** "Because they work as a team, they learn to share, and then they can enjoy the food together."
3. "If you could add one more fruit to their fruit salad, what would you choose? Why?"
**A:** "I would add mangoes / strawberries / grapes, because they are sweet and juicy."

---

### (5/5) `pick up` `at the end of the day`
#### 💬
1. "Grandma Hen asks, 'How was camp?' Before Lola answers, what do you think her face tells us?"
**A:** "She looks tired but happy — she had a full and fun day."
2. (推断性) "Lola says 'Yes' right away when Grandma asks if she wants to go back tomorrow. What does that tell us about her first day at camp?"
**A:** "It tells us she loved camp — she made friends, tried new things, and had a great time."
3. "Lola tried singing, painting, and cooking at camp. If you went to this camp, which activity would you like to try first? Why?"
**A:** "I would like to try painting / cooking first, because I am good at it / I want to learn something new."

## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `summer camp` 夏令营
2. `introduce ourselves` 自我介绍（oneself 随主语变化：myself / yourself）
3. `arts-and-crafts area` 手工区
4. `make a snack` 做点心
5. `fruit salad` 水果沙拉
6. `pick up` 接（人）； pickup 为名词"接人"
7. `at the end of the day` 一天结束的时候

### 二、核心句型
1. `I like to + 动词` / `I like + 动词-ing` 我喜欢……（文中两种形式都出现，意义基本一致）
2. `It's time to + 动词` 到……的时间了
3. `Would you like to + 动词?` 你想要……吗？

### 三、语法知识点
1. like to do 与 like doing
   - 例句：I like to sing. / I like singing too.（都表示"喜欢唱歌"，可以换用）
2. 一般过去时
   - 例词：was, asked, found, began, said, picked
   - 例句：Lola Chick was excited to go to summer camp for the first time.
3. who 引导的定语从句
   - 例句：...said Leo, who was sitting next to Lola Chick.（who 指 Leo，补充说明"坐在 Lola 旁边的"）

### 四、故事内容提问
1. Who is the main character of the story?（故事的主角是谁？）
   **A:** Lola Chick.
2. What did the campers do first at camp?（营员们第一件事做什么？）
   **A:** They sang a song to introduce themselves.
3. What did Lola do at the arts-and-crafts area?（Lola 在手工区做了什么？）
   **A:** She found an easel and began painting.
4. What did a few of the campers make together?（几个营员一起做了什么？）
   **A:** A fruit salad.
5. Who picked Lola up at the end of the day?（谁在一天结束时来接 Lola？）
   **A:** Grandma Hen.
6. Does Lola want to go back to camp tomorrow?（Lola 明天还想再去吗？）
   **A:** Yes, she does.

## 🎤 雅思口语对话练习（联系生活）

> 用法：老师当"考官"逐题发问，学生先口头回答，再对照 Model Answer 复盘。答案不唯一，鼓励换成自己的真实经历；每题答完可追加一句细节或例子。

**Part 1 · 个人经历（短问答，2–3 句）**

**Examiner:** Do you like summer camps? Why or why not?
**You:** "Yes, I do. I like camps because I can try new activities and make friends outside of school. / No, not really. I prefer quiet holidays at home, because camps can be noisy and tiring."

**Examiner:** Have you ever been to a summer camp? What did you do there?
**You:** "Yes, I went to a nature camp two years ago. We hiked, made a campfire, and slept in tents. It was the first time I cooked outdoors."

**Examiner:** Who would you like to go to a camp with — your friends or your family? Why?
**You:** "I would go with my friends, because we have the same hobbies and always have fun together. But I would also like one family camp, because my parents can take photos and cook for us."

**Part 3 · 深度讨论（观点 + 理由 + 例子，4–5 句）**

**Examiner:** What can children learn from summer camps that they cannot learn at school?
**You:** "Camps teach teamwork and independence. At school we mostly learn from books, but at camp you have to solve real problems — like putting up a tent or sharing jobs in a team. You also learn to get along with people day and night."

**Examiner:** Do you think schools should organize more outdoor activities? Why?
**You:** "Yes, I think so. Many students sit in classrooms too much, and outdoor activities give them exercise and fresh air. They also help shy students open up, because games make it easier to talk to each other."

**Examiner:** Some parents say camps are just playing, not studying. What do you think?
**You:** "I partly disagree. Playing is also a kind of learning — children practice communication, courage, and problem-solving. Of course, a good camp should have learning goals too, like English games or science experiments, so the time is not wasted."

**Plan B · 学生说 "I've never been to a summer camp" 时的备用问法**

> 策略：不纠缠经历，立刻转假设——"Let's imagine..." / "Would you like to..."。先教学生一句过渡（见下方小贴士），再从"意愿、想象、观点"出题。

*Part 1 备用（意愿 / 想象）*

**Examiner:** Would you like to go to a summer camp? Why / why not?
**You:** "Yes, I'd love to. I want to try sleeping in a tent and making new friends. / No, I don't think so, because I prefer quiet holidays with my family."

**Examiner:** If you could design your own camp, what kind would it be? What would people do there?
**You:** "I would design a sports-and-games camp. People would play basketball, run relay races, and have a water fight on the last day."

**Examiner:** What do you usually do in the summer holiday?
**You:** "I usually do my homework in the morning and swim or ride my bike in the afternoon. Sometimes my family goes to the beach for a few days."

*Part 3 备用（观点型，不依赖经历）*

**Examiner:** Why do you think some children never go to summer camps?
**You:** "Maybe because camps can be expensive, or their parents are too busy to take them. Some families also prefer tutoring or online classes during the holiday."

**Examiner:** Would you send your future child to a summer camp? Why?
**You:** "Yes, I would. Camps teach teamwork and independence, which are hard to learn from books. But I would choose a safe camp with good teachers first."

**Examiner:** Do you think a two-week camp is better than a two-month online course? Why?
**You:** "It depends on the child. A camp is better for social skills and health, while an online course helps with grades. Maybe a balance of both is the best."

**考官追问（练完可加练）**
- Why do you think so?
- Can you give me an example?
- Has it always been like this?

**地道表达小贴士**（老师旁白，不念给学生）
- `I partly disagree...` 我不完全同意……
- `give them exercise and fresh air` 让他们锻炼身体、呼吸新鲜空气
- `help shy students open up` 帮助害羞的学生放开自己
- `make it easier to do sth.` 让做某事变得更容易
- `get along with people` 与人相处

**"没经历过"万能过渡句**（Plan B 专用，考前必会）
- `I've never been to one, but I'd love to try.` 我没参加过，但很想试试。
- `I haven't had the chance yet, but I imagine it would be...` 我还没机会，但我想那会……
- `To be honest, I've never thought about it, but I think...` 说实话我没想过，但我觉得……
