## 📚 语言知识点

### 一、词组、固定搭配
1. `fly a kite`：放风筝
2. `there's no wind`：没有风
3. `run home`：跑回家
4. `take a break / take a little break`：休息一下
5. `take a nap`：小睡一会儿
6. `in the grass under a tree`：在树下草地上
7. `come back / return`：回来（until the wind returns / when the wind came back）

### 二、语法知识点
1. `Bert and Beth loved to fly their kite.` — love to do sth，love 后接带 to 的不定式。
2. `A kite won't fly.` — won't (will not) 表示"不会/不能"，讲风筝的客观情况。
3. `So Bert and Beth took a nap...` — so 表示"于是/所以"，连接因果。
4. 动词过去式：loved, ran, said, took, came, flew；特殊变化 run→ran, take→took, come→came, fly→flew。
5. `until the wind returns`：until 表示"直到……为止"。

### 三、故事内容提问
1. What do Bert and Beth love to do?
   **A:** "They love to fly their kite."
2. Why won't the kite fly?
   **A:** "Because there's no wind."
3. (推断性) What does Grandpa say about the wind?
   **A:** "He says maybe the wind just wants a little break — they should rest too, until the wind returns."
4. Where did Bert and Beth take a nap?
   **A:** "In the grass under a tree."
5. How does the story end?
   **A:** "The wind came back, and their kite flew again."
6. How do Bert and Beth feel on page 3 and on page 7?
   **A:** "Sad first because the kite can't fly; happy later because the wind comes back."
